## What

STATUS.md evidence-based feature matrix; docs/bugs_solved root-cause archive.

## How

Update STATUS when a feature is proven, not when the PR lands.

## Why

Stops aspirational docs from lying about shipping quality.

## Contracts

- Sources of truth: STATUS.md
- Sources of truth: docs/bugs_solved

## Notes

STATUS.md is the source of truth for what is proven vs experimental.

docs/bugs_solved archives root-cause writeups (NRD pack, OOM, etc.).
