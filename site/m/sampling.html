Sampling & MIS

Chapter contract

How randomness is produced and combined in shaders/rt/path_tracer.slang so light, environment and BSDF sampling stay unbiased and lower variance. Both RT profiles run this one tracer.

Monte Carlo baseline

\[ \Big\langle I\Big\rangle=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{f(X_i)}{p(X_i)} \]
Jargon · unbiased

Expected value equals the true integral when \(p>0\) wherever \(f\neq0\). Fireflies are high variance, not necessarily bias. The firefly clamp, applied only when the trace does not accumulate, trades a little bias for stability.

Why not pure random forever

Variance falls like \(1/\sqrt N\). Better \(p\) (BSDF sampling, the environment CDF, next-event estimation) and MIS cut the constant — that is this chapter’s job.

Owen-scrambled Sobol, in groups of four

Every raygen invocation (shaders/sampling/sobol.slang)
  1. Sampler rng = Sampler(pc.params.z, pcg(pixel.x ^ pcg(pixel.y ^ 0x7F4A7C15u))): the sample's index in the pixel's sequence, and a seed hashed from the pixel
  2. Draws come in groups of four: a group is one point of the 4-D Sobol sequence (Joe-Kuo direction numbers, dimension 0 the bit reversal), its index shuffled and its coordinates Owen-scrambled (Burley 2020, Vegdahl's constants) by hashCombine(pixelSeed, group)
  3. Group 0 is the camera jitter; bounce d takes group 1+3d for the light choice and point, 2+3d for the environment sample, 3+3d for the BSDF sample and Russian roulette (rng.group(g))
  4. Each coordinate is 24 bits as a float in [0, 1)
Why groups

The draws of one decision are jointly stratified because they share a point; different groups are independent because their scrambles are seeded apart. A pixel's samples across frames are one sequence, so they stay stratified however many there are. Measured on the closed forms at 64 samples a pixel: closed-box RMSE 0.065 against 0.104 for a PCG stream, furnace 0.0112 against 0.0322. The CPU twin (tools/sampling/, the module compiled to C++ by slangc) lets sobol_test check the shader bit for bit against sobol_generator.cpp and owen_scramble.cpp.

BSDF sampling (used in MIS)

OpenPBRBase::sample (shaders/material/openpbr.slang) picks the specular lobe with probability its share of the albedo's luminance, else the diffuse; GGX directions come from bounded VNDF sampling (Eto & Tokuyoshi 2023), diffuse from EON's sampler. OpenPBRBase::pdf returns the mixture's density for any direction, which is what the light side of MIS compares against.

MIS workflow

Two strategies, one integral
  1. Next-event estimation samples a light (or the environment) → direction ω, density \(p_L\)
  2. Evaluate the BSDF's \(p_f(\omega)\) at the same direction; weight \(p_L^2/(p_L^2+p_f^2)\)
  3. The BSDF-sampled bounce ray that meets the same light's emitter mesh (or escapes to the sky) is weighted \(p_f^2/(p_f^2+p_L^2)\)
  4. A sun (a delta) and a light without an emitter mesh are found by light sampling alone: full weight
\[ w_A^{\mathrm{pow}}=\frac{p_A^{2}}{p_A^{2}+p_B^{2}} \]
path_tracer.slang
float powerHeuristic(float a, float b)
{
    const float a2 = a * a, b2 = b * b;
    return a2 + b2 > 0.0 ? a2 / (a2 + b2) : 0.0;
}
Why MIS at all

Environment sampling alone misses sharp BSDF peaks; BSDF sampling alone misses sun texels and small lights. MIS lets each strategy win where it is strong, and counts every light once at any roughness.

Env CDF: build → upload → sample

CPU (env_cdf.cpp)
  1. Load HDR equirect; for each texel compute weight ∝ luminance × solid-angle factor
  2. Per row: prefix sums → conditional CDF; across rows → marginal CDF
  3. Upload float arrays → descriptor bindings 17 (marginal), 18 (conditional)
GPU (path_tracer.slang: sampleEnv, envPdf)
  1. Binary search the marginal → row y; the row's conditional → column x
  2. A point uniform inside texel (x, y) → direction (θ, φ), row 0 the zenith
  3. envPdf(dir): the texel's CDF steps × W × H over \(2\pi\cdot\pi\sin\theta\), for both sides of MIS
\[ p(\omega)=p_{uv}(u,v)\Big/\big(2\pi^{2}\sin\theta\big) \]
No-env contract

Without an environment (or a CDF of width 0) envSampled() is false: no environment sample is drawn and escaped rays take full weight.
