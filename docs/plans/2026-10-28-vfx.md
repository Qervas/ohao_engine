# VFX and transparency (Sub-project 12) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** In `blockout_play.scene.json`, and in every host, the fight has:
- a muzzle effect per round;
- an impact effect per surface, and a projected decal where each round hits;
- tracers showing where fire comes from;
- a grenade boom with lit, soft smoke that dims the room's RT GI bounce light from the doorway.

Every effect comes from spawn records the step writes, the same in a window and a headless host,
through restores, restarts and replays. One sorted forward pass draws particles, tracers and glTF
`BLEND` materials, lit by the clustered lights; `MASK` cuts in raster and RT. The set is data
(`assets/vfx/slice.vfx.json`), the VFX cost is gated, and the transparency, particle and decal
goldens are exact.

**Architecture:**
- **The step** (`ecs/vfx`): a simulate system after `combat` and the animation step, before
  `audio_schedule`. It turns the combat log (after the step's last `restart`) and placed
  `VfxEmitter`s into **spawn records** (effect, seed, spawn step, pose; tracers; decals with
  `impact_marks`' placement). Records are capped and dropped oldest-first, ride the World's
  `SnapshotHook`, and are never saved or hashed.
- **The data:** one `.vfx.json` per game (textures and flipbooks, effects of emitters, decals,
  tracers, event bindings, caps), named by the saved singleton `VfxSet`; placed `VfxEmitter`
  components and the level hint `vfx`.
- **The frame** (`render/vfx/`):
  - a `vfx` sync system uploads the records;
  - `vfx.expand` evaluates each particle in closed form at age `(step − spawn − 1 + alpha)·dt` and
    lights it per particle (clustered lights, one sun shadow ray, ambient);
  - `vfx.sort` orders objects back to front (and particles inside alpha emitters);
  - `transparent` draws them after SSR and before bloom, with a TAA transparency mask;
  - `decals` projects boxes into the G-buffer before lighting;
  - smoke records publish density spheres that `shadow_trace` and `gi_trace` attenuate through.
- **Materials:** `MaterialDesc` alpha mode, cutoff and opacity. `MASK` discards in the G-buffer and
  runs any-hit or non-opaque candidate handling in RT; `BLEND` goes forward.
- **Removed:** `ohao/render/particles/*`, `shaders/particles/*`, `runtime/combat_fx.*`, and the
  impact marks' quads.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, EnTT, Vulkan 1.3 (ray query, compute), Slang for every new shader (pinned
`slangc`), simdjson (the `.vfx.json` reader), Python 3 with numpy (texture and flipbook generators in
`tools/vfx/`, harnesses).

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-28-vfx-design.md`. It is binding once Frank accepts it; its
PROVISIONAL rulings 1–9 are designed to, and its OPEN rulings 10–27 are the recommendations until
ruled.

## Review Focus

1. **A round restart in the same step as a burst of hits:** the abandoned timeline's records are
   gone, the restored step's are written, and the next capture shows none of the old ones (Task 1,
   `t31_replay`; Task 2, `t32_capture_any_history`).
2. **Two transparent objects at exactly equal depth, and the camera inside a smoke cloud:** the same
   draw order every run, near-plane particles culled without flicker (Task 2, `t32_sort_order`).
3. **A decal box crossing the view model or a light's emitter mesh, and a face at exactly the 60°
   threshold:** nothing drawn on the gun or the lamp, the threshold inclusive the same way every run
   (Task 3, `t33_decal_wraps`).
4. **Every cap reached in one step** (a magazine into a wall plus a grenade): the oldest of each
   kind dropped and counted, the same records every run, no cost spike (Task 1, `t31_caps`; Task 4,
   the budget).
5. **A `MASK` texel exactly at the cutoff:** the G-buffer, the shadow ray, the GI ray and the path
   tracer agree (Task 2, `t32_mask_rays`).
