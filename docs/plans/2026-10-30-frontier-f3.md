# Frontier F3: emission is light

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md`, section 3 (step F3) and ruling F-R7 (both renderers
sample emissive triangles as lights, by power, through one table; proxies go).
**Status:** built 2026-10-30 (`docs/changes/2026-10-30-frontier-f3.md`); the design below is as built.

## Before F3

- **The path tracer** finds an emissive surface only when a BSDF-sampled bounce happens to hit it (weight 1). A
  small bright emitter (the helmet's visor, a lamp's shade) is found rarely: its light arrives as noise.
- **The deferred frame** adds an `EmissiveProxyLight` per model with an emissive texture: a point light of
  intensity `min(power x 0.1, 20)` at the mesh's box centre (`render::emissiveProxy`). RT GI's bounce rays already
  bring back the emission of what they hit, so a model's emission lights its room twice in deferred, once from a
  made-up point and once from the surface.

## Design

**One table of emissive triangles, built on the GPU every frame** (RTXDI's light preparation):
- The CPU keeps the set of instances with an emissive material (visible, not a light's emitter mesh or a view
  model), tracked where alpha instances are (`stageTlasInstance`), and hands the path tracer their ranges: slot,
  first light index, triangle count.
- `emissive.lights` (compute): one thread a triangle of those instances writes a triangle light (world corners,
  uvs, material) and its power `pi x luminance(emission_color x emission_luminance x the emissive texture's mean)
  x area` into level 0 of a power image (R32F, a power-of-two square), then the levels above sum 2x2 down to one
  texel: the total (`rt/emissive_collect.slang`, `rt/emissive_reduce.slang`). The texture's mean is one sample at
  the triangle's uv centroid at the mip whose texels match its uv area: without it the helmet's whole surface,
  most of it black in the emissive map, drew samples away from the point lights.
- **Selection by power without a scan:** a draw descends the levels, choosing among four children in proportion
  to their power (hierarchical warping, as RTXDI's local light PDF map). Its probability is its texel over the top.
- **The path tracer's next-event estimation** draws the analytic lights (uniformly, as before) with probability `q`
  and the emissive triangles with `1 - q`, `q` the analytic lights' power over all the power
  (`RenderScene::analyticLightPower`; a fixed 1/2 was noisier on the helmet), a point uniformly on the triangle
  seen from the shadow ray's origin, its
  emission with its texture, one shadow ray, two-sided as the closest hit is. A bounce ray that meets an emissive
  triangle is weighed against that sampling (power heuristic) through a per-instance first light index, so every
  emitter is counted once at any roughness.
- **The deferred frame:** the proxy goes. Its emissive light reaches surfaces through RT GI's bounce (the hit's
  emission), as it does in the path tracer; without ray queries emission lights nothing (ruling F-R6: the fallback
  is kept correct, not extended).

## Tests

- **The referee holds:** `check_material`'s closed box (every wall emissive, `Le / (1 - rho)`) with emissive
  triangles sampled; the furnace unchanged.
- **An emissive quad is an area light:** `check_analytic` renders a floor under an emissive rectangle (a mesh) and
  under an area light of the same radiance and shape; the path tracer holds both within 5% of the closed form.
- **Less noise where it should be:** the helmet room at 16 samples against 1024, the error near the visor before
  and after.
- **Deferred:** the helmet's frame without its proxy; its goldens move (the proxy's light leaves).
