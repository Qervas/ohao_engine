# Frontier F1: one path tracer

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md`, section 3 (F1), rulings F-R1, F-R2 and F-R3, and the
corrections in 4a.
**Goal:** the realtime profile runs `shaders/rt/path_tracer.slang`, the OpenPBR reference tracer, at budgeted
settings. The GLSL tracer and everything only it uses are deleted. Indirect light past the first bounce comes from a
hash-grid radiance cache. DLSS Ray Reconstruction works on NVIDIA.

## Tasks

### T1. The realtime profile on the reference tracer

- **Shader set.** `RTRealtimeRenderer` takes `PathTracerShaderSet{}` (the Slang entries).
- **Accumulation.** Both profiles reset accumulation when the view changes. A moving realtime view is one sample a
  frame, and the denoiser carries history (NRD's or DLSS RR's own, reprojected by their motion vectors).
- **Realtime defaults:**
  - external denoiser allowed, NRD by default (vendor neutral);
  - the firefly clamp stays;
  - `maxBounces` 2 until T3's cache.
- **Deleted:**
  - shaders: `pt_raygen_realtime.rgen`, `pt_closesthit.rchit`, `pt_miss.rmiss`, `pt_anyhit.rahit`,
    `includes/pbr_unpack.glsl`, `includes/material/ggx_aniso.glsl`, `includes/rt/{mis, env_sampling, sampler_api,
    sampler_pcg, sampler_sobol, sampler_sobol_tables, pt_lights}.glsl`, `rt/pt_spatial_denoise.comp`;
  - `render/rt/denoise/spatial_denoise.*` and the internal-denoise flag;
  - the realtime-only images: ReSTIR GI's reservoirs (bindings 29–34) and the surface and shading histories (13–16);
  - the dead settings `anisotropyStrength`, `anisotropyRotation`, `subsurfaceStrength` and `samplesPerFrame` (the
    Slang tracer traces one path a dispatch);
  - `IRTRenderPipeline` and the `RTRealtimePipeline`/`RTOfflinePipeline` metadata beside `rt_settings.hpp`.
- **Kept:**
  - `includes/rt/nrd_frontend.glsl`, which `nrd_compose.comp` uses;
  - `includes/scene/scene_data.glsl`, which the G-buffer uses;
  - binding 35, the specular hit distance, which DLSS RR reads. T4 has the Slang tracer write it.
- **Tests:**
  - the realtime goldens (`cornell_box_rt_realtime`, `cli_cornell_box_rt_realtime`) re-captured after looking;
  - `check_live`'s realtime cases;
  - `check_validation`'s realtime cases, NRD included;
  - `deferred_determinism_gpu_test`'s realtime Cornell.
  - New: a realtime frame and an offline frame of the same scene at one sample converge to the same mean (a
    still view, 256 frames each, within the noise). One tracer, one answer.

### T2. Owen-scrambled Sobol in the tracer

- Burley 2020's hash-based Owen scrambling of the Sobol sequence (4 dimensions a bounce, padded by per-pixel
  shuffling), replacing the PCG stream in `path_tracer.slang`, so both profiles sample alike.
- `ohao/render/rt/sobol_generator.*` and `owen_scramble.*` are rewritten as the CPU twin the test compares against, or
  deleted if the Slang code needs no table.
- Test: `sobol_test` checks the GPU's first points against the CPU twin. A furnace and closed-box run converge at least
  as fast as PCG (error at 64 samples a pixel, measured).

### T3. The radiance cache (SHaRC's design, OHAO's code)

- `shaders/rt/radiance_cache.slang`, a camera-relative hash grid with logarithmic levels:
  - 2^22 entries by default, 8-byte keys;
  - accumulation in `uint4` (radiance × scale, sample count); no float atomics;
  - resolved radiance at 16 bytes.
- **Update.** A raygen entry traces a sparse, frame-rotating subset of pixels (1 in 5×5) as segments, each adding its
  radiance to the entry of the vertex it left.
- **Resolve.** A compute entry blends each entry across frames up to a maximum count and evicts entries unseen for
  `staleFrames`.
- **Query.** The realtime raygen terminates a path into the cache at its second vertex or later, when the segment is
  longer than the voxel and the lobe's spread is wider than the voxel. The offline profile never queries the cache:
  it stays the unbiased reference.
- `render.settings` gets `rt.cache` (on/off, entries, staleFrames). `render.stats` gets the cache's occupancy.
- **Tests:**
  - the closed box (Le = 1, ρ = 0.5) in the realtime profile at 2 bounces with the cache converges toward
    Le/(1 − ρ) = 2 (within 5%, against 1.75 without it);
  - a still scene's occupancy settles below 50%;
  - two runs are bit-identical (integer accumulation);
  - the furnace stays 1 within 1%.
- **Bench:** `render_bench` times `rt.cache.update` and `rt.cache.resolve` on Cornell and the helmet, with proposed
  budgets.

**As built (2026-10-30):** 2^21 entries (72 MiB) and 32-bit keys with 8 probes (a key collision
merges two cells, rare at this size), so no 64-bit atomics are needed. `render.settings rt.cache` and
the occupancy in `render.stats` are not built; they go with T6's per-pass timings. The realtime closed
box reaches 2 within 3% (1.972 at 256 frames), and realtime Cornell is within 0.3% of the reference.

### T4. DLSS Ray Reconstruction

- `tools/fetch_dlss.py` downloads NVIDIA's DLSS SDK release into the git-ignored `external/DLSS` and checks it against a
  pinned hash. `dlss_rr.cpp` links the Windows `nvsdk_ngx` library (today it looks only for the Linux one).
- The Slang tracer writes the specular hit distance (binding 35) and the guides DLSS RR reads.
- `--denoise dlss` on the realtime profile is validated: `check_validation`, a window run, and a `check_live` case
  where the device has it, skipped otherwise.
- Attribution: the DLSS mark in `ohao_game`'s credits text and in the README, per the license supplement.

### T6. Real time at 1080p

Measured after T3 (GPU ms for one frame at 1920x1080): Cornell 7.6, the helmet room 44, the play
scene 58. Direct light alone on the helmet costs 44 ms, so the cost is in the primary hit, not the
bounces. Sobol is about 10% of it (PCG: 39.9). Candidates, each measured before and after:
- Ray-cone texture LOD (Akenine-Möller et al. 2021, "Improved Shader and Texture Level of Detail
  Using Ray Cones"): the hit shaders sample every texture at LOD 0.
- Shader execution reordering (`VK_EXT_ray_tracing_invocation_reorder` / NV): sort hits by
  material before shading.
- A smaller payload (it carries every OpenPBR input).
- `sobolBits` by Gray code or tables.
- `pt.render` split into `pt.cache.update`, `pt.cache.resolve` and `pt.trace` timers, with
  `render_bench` scenes for the realtime profile and budgets proposed.

## Order and risk

- T1 first: it deletes the second tracer and makes the realtime profile the reference's code.
- T2 before T3, so the cache is judged with the final sampler.
- T4 last: it needs an outside download and is NVIDIA-only.
- **Risk:** T1 changes what a realtime window looks like (no reprojected internal history). NRD becomes the realtime
  default to carry the history, so a realtime window without a denoiser is noisier in motion than today. That is
  stated in the change record.
