# Tidewall: the mission demo, its plan

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-18-tidewall-design.md` (all sections; the modules' shape from
`docs/specs/2026-11-18-engine-modules-design.md`, R4-R7). **Status:** planned 2026-11-18 (documents only), to start
after step 4b's close (`docs/plans/2026-11-18-step4b-jobs.md:568`), on a branch of its own. Frank's rulings F1-F5 are
the spec's; what waits on him is under "Needs Frank".

The mission adds eight engine modules (`navigation`, `perception`, `behavior`, `tactics`, `ai`, `mission`,
`destruction`, `doors`), moves two out of the slice (`damage`, `weapons`), adds `ui`, and builds one game,
`games/tidewall`, whose 13 minutes pull every one of them. Each part ends with the engine running, the hooks green and
its numbers measured; the first thing seen moving is enemies pathing through the yard's blockout, at part N1.

## The requirement, questioned first

- **"Ten modules for one demo."** Each serves a beat (spec section 2) and is a feature another shooter needs whole; the
  standard's alternative, code in the game, is the code the next game writes again (R4). Not built: bullet
  penetration, melee, vehicles, cutscenes, saves on disk, streaming, gamepad (spec section 4), each with its reason.
- **"A whole level before the modules are proven."** No: the yard (60 x 40 m) comes first and carries N1 to A3; the rest
  of the level is laid out only once the yard's fight holds its budget.
- **"High quality everywhere."** No (F4): one room, last, when the systems it shows are settled; its assets are
  Frank's and stay local.
- **"A new gate for the mission."** No gate is added for a feature (AGENTS.md "Gates"). The mission proves itself by
  measurement, by the planted bugs below, and by the existing `replay_test` discipline run by hand on its scenes. A
  gate is added only if a real bug gets through.

## Where it starts

Facts from the tree at `8694c1cd` (spec section 1 has the full table):
- No navigation: enemies walk straight at the last seen place (`games/slice/enemy_ai.cpp:215-220`); no hearing; sight
  by one ray in a cone while idle (`enemy_ai.cpp:149-163`).
- The slice's weapon and health are game code (`games/slice/components.hpp:18-62`, `combat.cpp`, 603 lines); the test
  grenade does no damage (`combat.cpp:355-395`).
- Physics gives a game `raycast` and `raycastFrom` only (`ohao/physics/physics_scene.hpp:200-203`); shape casts,
  overlaps and layers are private (`physics_backend.hpp:516-529, 71-90`).
- Breakable joints exist (`ohao/physics/components.hpp:62-84`); no fracture, debris or ragdoll (`docs/ROADMAP.md:74`).
- The HUD's font is 5x7, upper case and digits (`ohao/render/hud/hud_list.hpp:38-41`); no menu.
- One level, `blockout_room.glb` (`assets/levels/README.md:10`); the play scene's frame 8.7 ms at 1080p, scale 1, 4.7
  at 0.667 (`docs/notes/renderer.md:549-550`); the crowd's 64 enemies step in about 1 ms at 8 workers
  (`docs/plans/2026-11-18-step4b-jobs.md:365-368`).

## Global constraints

- **One feature, one module, additive by proof** (R4, R5): a part touches its module's directory and the module list;
  `check_layers.py` proves each new module deletable; at the close each new leaf module is removed and the engine
  built and gated without it.
- **Determinism:** every new system declares its access (4b-4); the same `world.hash` at 1 thread and 31, waves
  reversed (`OHAO_WAVE_ORDER=reverse`), from a checkpoint and in a new process. No float added atomically; agents in
  `parallelFor` chunks write their own entity only.
- **The entity set is fixed at load** (spec 3.4, 3.5): actors and fragments are parked, never created or destroyed in
  play.
- **Bounded:** every query, tree and fracture has caps in its file's schema, refused at load. No GPU work is unbounded;
  the navmesh cook runs on the pool's background priority.
- **The machine:** benches headless; one visible window run per part at most; temperatures before and after the full
  benches.
- **Content:** Frank's assets stay in `assets/local/`; every committed asset is ours or CC0 with its licence file.
- **Budgets** are added with their measurement (spec section 6); none is loosened.

## The referee

- **Noise first:** each bench 3 alternated rounds after a warm-up, the spread recorded before a difference is called.
- **The slice holds:** `replay_test`, the goldens and every existing bench at each part, unchanged except the one
  named change of W1.
- **Tidewall's own replay** (by hand at each part from M1): a recorded run of the beats built so far (`input.record`),
  replayed in a new process and from each checkpoint, at `--threads 1` and 31 with the waves reversed: equal hashes at
  every checkpoint.
- **The frame:** `render_bench tidewall_<beat>_1080p` at each beat's fixed view (written from the layout's `view`
  entries, as `crowd_1080p` is written by its bench).

- **Visible:** walk the yard (one window run).
- **As built (2026-11-19, branch `tidewall`):**

    `assets/tidewall/scenes/yard.scene.json`: the player at the south entry, a moon, the environment, the yard.

  a 640x360 deferred capture from the spawn, 1.24 ms GPU. A player walks from the spawn through the 2 m south entry,
  20 m in 300 steps (`input.inject`). **Owed** (Frank's GPU rule, 2026-11-19): `asset_bench`'s load to first frame,
  `render_bench tidewall_yard_1080p` at scales 1 and 0.667, the window walk.

## What gets deleted

## Risks

- **Recast's floats change with a compiler upgrade:** the navmesh's cook key includes the cooker version; replay holds
  within one build, as Jolt's does.
- **The AI's step cost at 24 agents** is an estimate: if A3 misses 2.0 ms, the stagger (every 4 steps) and the query
  caps are the levers, measured, before any budget talk.
- **Content is the long pole:** crouch and cover clips, voice, the hero room's props; each has a stand-in so no part
  waits on it.
- **W1's hash change** is a deliberate break of every recorded hash; done once, at W1, before anything else records
  Tidewall hashes.
