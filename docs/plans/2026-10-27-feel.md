# Feel (Sub-project 9) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On `blockout_play.scene.json` in `ohao_game`, a mouse movement reaches the screen within 15 ms (median, poll to present, 144 Hz, vsync off), measured and reported; the camera turns at render rate while the step's aim stays authoritative; frames come evenly (p99 ≤ 1.5 × median); the gun sways, bobs, kicks and never clips a wall; hits throw sparks; sounds attenuate, cap and occlude; SP7's leftovers and Frank's hand-test items are closed.

**Architecture:**
- Measure first: `LiveHost` stamps each frame (poll, the step that consumed its look, view resolve, submit, present through `VK_KHR_present_wait`/`present_id`, else the fence), keeps a ring of frame records, and reports `latency` and `pacing` through `render.stats`; `feel_bench` turns them into budgets.
- The look lead: the host hands the World the look the steps have not consumed (`World::setPendingLook`); a `look_lead` sync system after `interpolate` turns the input-driven player's and head's `RenderPose` by it through `applyLook`, the function the step itself uses, so the step lands where the frame already was. Presentation only: never saved, hashed or snapshotted; zero in lockstep and headless hosts.
- Pacing: present modes (`immediate`, `mailbox`, `fifo`) and late input sampling (wait for the next frame's slot before polling, `framesAhead`).
- Hand feel: `ViewModel` (saved parameters) and a `view_model` sync system (sway, bob, kick into the gun's `RenderPose`, a view-space remap for its field of view and depth); combat events to `DeferredRenderer::spawnParticles` in realtime windows only; distance curves, a voice cap and an occlusion ray in the window's sound players.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, EnTT, Vulkan 1.3 (`VK_KHR_present_wait`, `VK_KHR_present_id` where present), GLFW, miniaudio, Jolt (through `PhysicsScene`), Python 3 harnesses.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-27-feel-design.md`

## Review Focus

1. Two steps in one frame consuming a split batch of mouse events: the lead after the frame is the unconsumed remainder and the drawn view is continuous (Task 2, `t19_lead_split_across_steps`).
2. Pitch at the ±89° clamp with a lead pushing past it: the drawn pitch stops exactly where the step will (Task 2, `t19_lead_matches_next_step` clamp case).
3. A placement (restart, `player.teleport`) while look is pending: the drawn view jumps with the placement, the lead stays on top, nothing is interpolated across (Task 2, `t19_lead_across_placement`).
4. A realtime window switched to lockstep (`time.set`) with look pending: the lead is dropped with the events and the next capture equals headless bit for bit (Task 2, check_live `test_lead_dropped_in_lockstep`).
5. A restart mid-burst: the gun's kick and sway reset, no carry-over into the restored round (Task 3, `t21_view_model_resets_on_restart`).
