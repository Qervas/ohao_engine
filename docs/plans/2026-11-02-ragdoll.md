# Sub-project 13 — Joints and ragdoll: implementation plan

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-02-ragdoll-design.md`. Four build steps, each ending with the engine running, every gate
green and a change record (`docs/changes/YYYY-MM-DD-ragdoll-stepN.md`).

## Found while planning (spec corrections, written into the spec's "As built" at step 1's close)

- **Jolt orders constraints by index, and removal moves the last into the gap** (`ConstraintManager::Remove`); the
  solver sorts by priority, then index. Jolt's `RestoreState` (non-validating) restores by index. So constraint identity
  is the list of (handle, Jolt index, entity), not the set of handles.
- **Jolt saves each constraint's `mEnabled`** (`Constraint::SaveState`): a disabled ragdoll's constraints restore
  exactly (the spec's first risk is closed).
- **Jolt's local constraint space is relative to the centre of mass** (`EConstraintSpace::LocalToBodyCOM`), not the
  body's origin; a compound body's centre of mass is off it. The backend converts local anchors to world space with the
  body's own transform and hands Jolt world space.
- **A broken joint is disabled, not removed.** Removing it changes the constraint list mid-round, and a restore from
  before the break would fall back to teleports. Disabled, Jolt's state brings it back whole, bit for bit.

- [x] **Step 1: The backend.**
  - `ConstraintSettings.space` (`Local` default, `World`); local anchors and axes are turned into world space through the
    body's transform (the world's for an INVALID body2) and Jolt gets `WorldSpace`. Existing backend tests say `World`.
  - `CONE_TWIST` (Jolt's cone, no twist limit) is replaced by `SWING_TWIST` (`SwingTwistConstraint`: twist axis,
    plane axis, `swingNormalHalfCone`, `swingPlaneHalfCone`, `twistMin`, `twistMax`).
  - `setConstraintLimits` covers hinge, slider and swing-twist (its twist); others log a refusal once.
  - Breaking disables the constraint and reports it; `destroyConstraint` is the only removal.
  - `saveState`/`restoreState` carry the constraint table (sorted handles with their Jolt index); a restore whose table
    differs is refused, as a different character set is.
  - `physics_backend_tests`: local anchors on a rotated, offset compound body; swing-twist limits; a break disabling;
    a restore refused across a removed constraint and exact across a disabled one.
- [x] **Step 2: `Joint` and `JointState`.** Saved components with docs, units and limits: `Joint {type, other,
  anchor, axis, otherAnchor, otherAxis, hasOther, limitMin, limitMax, swingNormal, swingPlane, twistMin, twistMax,
  springHz, springDamping, breakForce, breakTorque}` (angles in degrees), `JointState {broken}`.
  - The `physics` sync builds a joint after the bodies it joins: this entity's body, else its nearest ancestor's;
    `other`'s likewise, or the world. Anchors are authored in the joint entity's space; the other side defaults to the
    same world point at build. Built disabled when `JointState.broken` is set.
  - A body destroyed or rebuilt first destroys its joints and queues them for rebuild after it; a `Joint` edited or
    removed rebuilds or removes its constraint. Warnings `W_JOINT_NO_BODY`, `W_JOINT_STATIC`, `W_JOINT_SELF`.
  - After a step's substeps, broken constraints write `JointState.broken` (the step) and a `joint_break` event.
  - `SavedSimulation` gains the constraint identity (handle, entity id); `restoreSaved` compares it.
  - `PhysicsInfo` and `physics.info` gain `constraints`, `brokenConstraints`.
- [x] **Step 3: The joints scene.** `assets/scenes/joints.scene.json` on the blockout: a door on a hinge (0–110°), a
  chain of 20 boxes on point joints from the ceiling, a lamp on a distance joint with a point light below it, a plank
  fixed at both ends that breaks under a dropped crate.
- [x] **Step 4: Test 36** (`level_physics_tests`, `t36_*`): the spec's list. **Bench:** `chain_step_ms` under a new
  `ragdollStep` key (pending until step 2 of the build sets the key).
- [x] **Step 5: Gates and the record.** `check.py`; the schema hash's moves in CLI and serve expectations (`--update`,
