# Frontier F4: direct light, resampled

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md`, section 2 ("Direct light") and section 3 (step F4):
ReSTIR DI in the hybrid frame with MegaLights' structure (sample, trace, shade, denoise), one shadow ray a sample,
any number of shadowed lights, area lights exact.
**Status:** built 2026-10-30 (`docs/changes/2026-10-30-frontier-f4.md`); F4b 2026-10-31 resamples and traces at half resolution (`docs/changes/2026-10-31-frontier-f4b.md`); three gated frame budgets missed, a ruling for Frank. As built: 4 candidates and 2 neighbours by default, a cheap RIS target, Z normalisation with visibility rays, a boiling filter, a 3x3 a-trous over three steps; the deferred frame shades at full resolution.

## Before F4

- **Shadowed direct light is a loop with a visibility image.** `rt.shadows` traces one ray to each of a pixel's 4
  most important lights (of the first 8 shadow-casting lights: `params.w` is a light's channel), `rt.shadows.filter`
  denoises the bits spatially and temporally into eight 8-bit channels, and `lighting` loops over the froxel's lights
  multiplying each by its channel. Lights past the 8th render unshadowed; pixels with more than 4 that matter drop
  some (`render.stats shadowLightsDropped`). Reference quality traces every light with 8 rays and averages frames.
- **Sphere and area specular are approximations** (the representative point, the widened lobe), and the shadow is
  filtered visibility multiplied onto unshadowed light, not shadowed light: a penumbra's shading is wrong where the
  visible part of an area light is not its centre.
- **Emissive surfaces light deferred only through RT GI's bounce** (F3): no direct emissive light.

## Design

**One light set, sampled by power.** The deferred frame samples what the path tracer samples:
- the shadow-casting raster lights (point, spot, area, directional), every one of them, by an alias table of their
  power (`RenderScene::analyticLightPower`'s terms, per light), built on the host with the light table;
- the emissive triangles of F3's table (`EmissiveLights`, moved from the path tracer to the renderer, recorded once a
  frame for both consumers);
- chosen between by power, as the path tracer chooses (`q`).

A sample is `(light, u, v)`: a point fixed by the light and two numbers (on a sphere's surface, a rectangle, a
triangle, or a direction in the sun's cone), so it is the same light point wherever a neighbour re-evaluates it.

**ReSTIR DI** (Bitterli et al. 2020), in compute passes with inline ray queries:
- `di.sample`: each pixel draws `candidates` (default 8) samples from the light set and keeps one by RIS against the
  target `p̂ = luminance(f x Le x G)` (OpenPBR's `eval`, unshadowed); one shadow ray to the kept sample (visibility
  reuse: an occluded sample keeps its M and loses its weight).
- `di.temporal`: the pixel's reservoir from last frame through the motion vectors (rejected on depth and normal),
  combined with this frame's, the history's M capped at 20 x this frame's.
- `di.spatial`: `spatial` (default 4) neighbours within 30 px that pass the same similarity tests, combined.
- `di.shade`: one shadow ray to the final sample (smoke proxies' transmittance on it, as `rt.shadows` had), its
  shadowed contribution `f x Le x G x V x W` split into diffuse and specular, each over its directional albedo
  (demodulated), into two RGBA16F images.
- `di.denoise`: a reprojected temporal accumulation and a depth- and normal-aware a-trous filter on each (RT GI's
  denoiser's structure, `rt/gi_denoise.slang`), which `lighting` remodulates by the albedos.
- **Reference quality**: `di.sample` with no reuse and `di.shade` keep the running mean of the frames since the last
  reset, no denoise: an unbiased estimate that converges (what `check_analytic` holds deferred to).
- **Deterministic:** every random number hashes (seed, frame since the reset, pixel, pass, dimension); no float
  atomics; a capture is the same bits from any frame slot.

**What the lighting loop keeps:** lights that cast no shadow (flashes, `castShadows: false`), whose clustered loop is
exact and cheap; and the fallback, every light, when RT direct light does not run (no `VK_KHR_ray_query`, or
`rtDirect` off), with CSM for a directional light (ruling F-R6). Transparent meshes and particles keep their forward
loop and their one sun ray; the sun is the first directional light that casts shadows (no channel).

**The API:** `render.settings rtShadows {enabled, maxLights}` becomes `rtDirect {enabled, candidates 1-32,
spatial 0-8}`; `render.stats` loses `shadowLightsDropped`.

**Deleted:** `rt/shadow_trace.slang`, `rt/shadow_filter.slang`, `RTShadowTechnique`, the shadow channels
(`LightGpu.params.w` as a channel, `kShadowMaxLights`, `kShadowLightsPerPixel`, `ShadowLight`,
`shadowCastersWithoutChannel`), `LightVisibility`'s RT words, `shadowLightsDropped`; the budgets on `rt.shadows`
and `rt.shadows.filter` go with their passes (replacements proposed, pending Frank's ruling).

## Tasks

- **T1** one light set: `EmissiveLights` owned by the renderer; the shadow-casting lights' power alias table; both
  bound to the DI passes and the path tracer.
- **T2** ReSTIR DI: the passes, the reservoir images (ping-pong), reference quality; `lighting` reads the result.
- **T3** the retirement: RT shadows, the channels, the API members; transparent and particle sun lookup.
- **T4** tests, budgets, goldens, docs, the change record.

## Tests

- **The referee:** `check_analytic` holds deferred (realtime, after its frames, and reference) within 5% under
  point, spot, sun, area and the emissive rectangle; `emissive_quad` deferred at realtime quality from RT direct
  light, no longer GI's x0.85.
- **Many shadowed lights:** a test with 64 shadow-casting lights behind a wall, each pixel's light from every
  unoccluded one; the old cap of 8 shadowed would light the occluded ones.
- **Parity:** `check_parity` (deferred against the path tracer in stops) no worse on any reference scene.
- **Determinism:** `deferred_determinism_gpu_test` exact; captures equal from every frame slot (`engine_tests`).
- **Noise:** Cornell's 12 lights and the helmet's emitters at realtime quality against reference, error before and
  after.
- **Cost:** `render_bench` per pass; budgets proposed for Cornell 720p and the 1,000-light bench scenes.
