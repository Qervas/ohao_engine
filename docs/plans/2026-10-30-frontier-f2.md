# Frontier F2: the display chain

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md`, section 3 (step F2) and rulings F-R4 (AgX by default,
PBR Neutral selectable) and F-R5 (a physical camera, EV100, histogram auto exposure in windows, captures at a fixed EV).
**Status:** T1 and T2 built (`docs/changes/2026-10-30-frontier-f2.md`).

## Today

- **Four tonemaps.** The deferred post's `tonemapping.frag` (ACES fit by default, plus Reinhard, Uncharted 2, a
  "filmic" curve and Khronos PBR Neutral by a push-constant switch nothing sets), the path tracer's `aces()`,
  `nrd_compose.comp` and `dlss_tonemap.comp`, and OIDN's CPU copy (`oidn_denoise.cpp`).
- **A gamma of 2.2,** `pow(x, 1/2.2)`, where the output is sRGB.
- **Two fixed exposures:** the deferred frame's 0.6 (`render.settings exposure`, a multiplier), the path tracer's 0.5.
- **A Preetham sky with a procedural night** (`postprocess/sky.frag`, `SkyPass`), drawn only without an HDRI. It
  lights nothing; a scene without an HDRI is lit by its lights and, without RT GI, a constant ambient of 0.35.
- **A thresholded bloom** clamped at luminance 4 before exposure (`bloom_threshold.frag`).

## Units

Lights stay in engine units (ruling O4): a directional light of intensity `I` gives irradiance `I`. The sky keeps
them: Hillaire's model is linear in the sun's illuminance, so the sky is computed for the scene's sun at its intensity
(no sun: a default, documented). Exposure is EV100 (Lagarde and de Rousiers 2014): a scene radiance `L` displays as
`L / (1.2 x 2^EV100)` before the tonemap. Today's 0.6 is EV100 0.47; the path tracer's 0.5 is 0.74.

## Tasks

### T1. One display transform

- `shaders/display/display.slang`: `exposureScale(ev100)`, AgX (Sobotka's AgX; the base curve as fit by Wrensch's
  minimal AgX: the inset matrix, log2 encoding over [-12.47, 4.03] EV, the polynomial sigmoid, the outset matrix,
  linearised for the sRGB step), Khronos PBR Neutral (the published reference), and the sRGB transfer function.
  One `display(radiance, ev100, curve)`.
- Every path uses it: the deferred post's tonemap (ported to Slang), the path tracer's output, `nrd_compose`, the
  DLSS display pass; OIDN's CPU path through the module's slangc C++ (as `tools/sampling` does for Sobol), so it
  cannot drift.
- `render.settings` and `render.capture`: `exposure` becomes `ev100` (default 0.5 for both renderers; the path tracer
  stops using its own 0.5) and `tonemap` (`agx` | `neutral`). The referee's PNGs (`check_material`,
  `check_analytic`, `check_parity`) stay in linear HDR; their PNG previews use `neutral`.
- **Retired:** the ACES fit (four copies and OIDN's), Reinhard, Uncharted 2, the "filmic" curve, `TonemapOperator`'s
  old values, `pow(1/2.2)`, `setGamma`, the fixed 0.5.
- **Tests:** the module on the CPU (a unit suite): AgX against reference values at known EVs, PBR Neutral
  identity below 0.76, the sRGB pair round-tripping, exposure at EV100 0 = 1/1.2; every golden re-captured and
  looked at (all move: a new curve).

### T2. Auto exposure

- A luminance histogram (compute, 64 bins of log2 luminance over [-10, 20] EV, the centre-weighted frame) and an
  adaptation pass (the mean of the bins between the 50th and 95th percentile, eased per frame, Lagarde's EV
  compensation): a GPU-resident EV100 the display pass reads, never read back.
- Windows only (`render.settings` gains `autoExposure` and `exposureCompensation` beside T1's `ev100`, the fixed
  value); captures always fixed, so goldens are deterministic.
- **Tests:** a scene twice as bright settles at 1 EV more; `engine_tests` holds a capture's bytes unchanged by auto
  exposure in the window.

### T3. Hillaire sky as the default environment

- Hillaire (EGSR 2020): transmittance LUT (256x64), multiple scattering LUT (32x32), sky-view LUT (192x108), the
  aerial perspective volume (32x32x32), for Earth's atmosphere (Rayleigh, Mie, ozone) and the scene's sun.
- **It lights through the HDRI's paths:** a scene without an HDRI renders the sky into the environment map (equirect,
  the same row-0-zenith convention), so the CDF, the prefilter, RT GI's misses and the path tracer's misses use it
  unchanged. Re-rendered when the sun moves. The sun's disc is not in the map (the directional light is the sun, as
  `env_sun` arranges for HDRIs).
- Aerial perspective in deferred lighting (the froxel volume) and on path-traced camera hits.
- `Environment` gains `sky: {atmosphere}` beside `hdri`; no `Environment` means the sky.
- **Retired:** `sky.frag` (Preetham and the procedural night), `SkyPass`'s Preetham parameters, the constant ambient
  (`ambientIntensity`, `setAmbientIntensity`): every scene has an environment.
- **Tests:** the transmittance LUT against the closed form for a pure-Rayleigh atmosphere at the zenith; the sky at
  noon bluer above than at the horizon, orange at sunset; `check_env_orientation` with the sky; the furnace and the
  analytic referee unchanged (they set their own environments).

### T4. Bloom by energy

- No threshold and no clamp: the frame's energy convolved by the dual-filter pyramid (Jimenez, SIGGRAPH 2014, "Next
  Generation Post Processing in Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare"), Karis's average on the first downsample, mixed at a
  small strength (0.04) before exposure, so a light's mesh glares by its radiance, not by a threshold.
- **Retired:** `bloom_threshold.frag`, `kBloomMaxLuminance`.
- **Tests:** bloom adds no energy (the frame's mean radiance within 0.1%); a 19,000-radiance emitter's glare stays
  inside a radius the test states.

### T5. Close

AGENTS.md (the display, exposure, sky and bloom paragraphs), ARCHITECTURE.md, the CLI and serve guides, the change
record, the goldens, budgets for the new passes (`exposure.histogram`, `sky.luts`).

## Order and risk

- T1 first: it moves every golden once, and every later task is judged through it.
- **Risk:** every image's look changes (AgX desaturates highlights where ACES skewed them). Each golden is looked at;
  the referee's linear numbers do not move.
- **Risk:** the sky replaces the black background of scenes without an HDRI, so they gain light (sky fill). That is
  the intended change; their goldens move again in T3.
