# Frontier F5: indirect light

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md`, section 3 (step F5): the radiance cache behind RT GI for
every bounce; ReSTIR GI on the first; RT reflections through the same paths. Retires one-bounce GI, SSAO in lighting
(the material's AO texture stays), SSR, the split sum for occluded specular, and the constant ambient (moved here by F2).
**Status:** done (2026-10-31): F5a, F5b and F5c built (`docs/changes/2026-10-31-frontier-f5a.md`, `-f5b.md`, `-f5c.md`), then the GI denoiser (`2026-10-31-gi-denoise.md`) and ReSTIR DI's spatial reuse (`2026-10-31-di-pairwise.md`) fixed. ## Before F5

- **RT GI is one bounce** (`rt/gi_trace.slang`): a cosine ray from the G-buffer surface; a hit returns its OpenPBR
  albedo over pi times one light's shadowed direct light (`directAt`, the analytic lights), plus its emission unless RT
  direct light samples emissive triangles. Nothing reaches the hit from other surfaces: the referee's remaining gap
  (Cornell 0.881 of the path tracer, the spheres 0.767; `check_parity`).
- **The radiance cache** (`rt/radiance_cache.slang`, `render/rt/radiance_cache.*`) is the path tracer's alone (realtime
  profile): its update is a raygen in the tracer's pipeline.
- **Specular indirect** is the split sum of the prefiltered environment times GI's environment visibility, GI's
  diffuse radiance for the occluded rest, weighed by `specularOcclusion` (material AO x SSAO), then SSR replaces it where
  its screen-space march hits (`lit += W (cL) - cE`). Without an environment there is no specular indirect at all.
- **SSAO** multiplies the constant ambient, the specular occlusion and the IBL irradiance without RT GI.

## Steps

- **F5a, every bounce through the cache.** RT GI owns a `RadianceCache` (the path tracer's class, its own instance). At
  each GI ray's hit (alpha-tested, its geometric normal from the triangle's vertices) one more cosine ray reads what the
  cache holds where it lands (if at least a voxel away) plus that surface's emission, or the environment: the hit's
  indirect light. The GI ray brings back albedo/pi x direct + albedo x that, and the same value is added to the hit's
  cell, so each frame carries light one bounce further (SHaRC's propagation through short paths, our code). The resolve
  (`rt.gi.cache`) blends the frame's samples; a temporal reset clears it. Emissive surfaces light GI's hits through the
  second ray (`directAt` sees analytic lights only).
- **F5b, RT reflections.** GI traces a specular ray (GGX VNDF, the surface's roughness) beside its diffuse rays, shaded
  the same way (direct at the hit, the cache behind it); lighting's specular indirect is that, denoised by roughness.
  Retires SSR (`ssr`, `ssr_resolve`), the split sum's visibility and `specularOcclusion` on RT frames, E/W.
- **F5c, the fills go.** SSAO (`ssao` pass, `render.settings ssao`, the post effect) and the constant ambient: without
  RT GI and without an environment there is no indirect light (as in the path tracer). The material's AO texture stays
  (it is authored).
- **F5d, ReSTIR GI** on the first bounce (Ouyang et al. 2021) if the referee still asks for it after F5a-c.

## Open after F5 (deferred, 2026-10-31)

Small, measured, none a visible artefact in the reference scenes; each has a test that would catch it getting worse.

- **Half-resolution RT direct light is about 1% under full resolution** on Sponza at night (0.987 against 0.995 of
  reference quality at frame 120): a sample's visibility is its trace pixel's.
- **RT GI is about 4% dark on Sponza at night** (its 18% share of the frame), and the blockout room is 0.961 of the path
  tracer (the other reference scenes 0.986-1.016).
- **Multi-bounce GI lags a lighting change**: sparse radiance cache cells take few samples a frame (the closed box 0.937
  of its closed form at 32 frames, 0.986 at 256).
- **GI's lag behind a small dimming has no test**: a light off or halved snaps within 4 frames (the history clamp); a
  smaller change fades over the 32-frame history (about 0.5 s).
- **Reflections reproject by surface motion**: a moving camera smears a mirror's reflection for a few frames.
- **`rt.gi` costs about 5 ms on Sponza at 1080p** (render_bench `sponza_night_1080p`, `sponza_1080p`; measured beside a
  game on the GPU, so unconfirmed), against 0.7 ms in the helmet room. Measure on a quiet machine first.
- **Budgets for Frank:** gated frame budgets missed since F5a/F5b (cornell_720p about 2.0 against 1.50 ms, play_hud_1080p
  4.1 against 2.45, play_vfx_1080p 3.1 against 2.79, all noisy runs); `rt.gi.denoise` on cornell_720p 0.38 against 0.20 ms
  (F5b's second chain, the GI denoiser's two a-trous steps); the Sponza scenes have none.
