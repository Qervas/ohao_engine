# Frontier F1: one path tracer

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md` (section 3, step F1; rulings F-R1, F-R2 and F-R3; the
corrections in 4a). **Plan:** `docs/plans/2026-10-30-frontier-f1.md`, T1–T6.
**Status:** built. Gate 54 of 54 at every task.

## 1. Summary

**The realtime profile now runs the reference tracer** (`shaders/rt/path_tracer.slang`, OpenPBR). The second, GLSL
realtime tracer is gone, with its pre-OpenPBR BRDF, its 2021 ReSTIR GI and its internal spatial denoiser.

**Every bounce reaches a realtime frame.** Paths end in a radiance cache of our own, built on SHaRC's design.

**Both profiles draw Owen-scrambled Sobol.**

**DLSS Ray Reconstruction works on Windows:**
- it is the realtime denoiser on NVIDIA, with NRD as the vendor-neutral fallback;
- every denoiser presents its frame through one display transform;
- two legacy "cinematic" post chains and the SVGF denoiser are deleted.

About 15,000 lines left the tree, against 2,500 added.

## 2. What was built

### T1: one tracer
- `RTRealtimeRenderer` takes the Slang shader set. The rt_meta template traits became one `constexpr` policy over
  `RTRenderSettings` (`tracerAccumulates`, `tracerAccumulatesAOVs`).
- **Two clocks.** The trace's running mean restarts when the view moves. A temporal denoiser's history restarts only
  at `resetAccumulation`, and reprojects through motion vectors written whenever last frame's view exists.
- The firefly clamp applies only when the trace does not accumulate.

**Deleted:**
- `pt_raygen_realtime.rgen` and the GLSL hit, miss and any-hit shaders;
- nine GLSL includes;
- `pt_spatial_denoise` and `SpatialDenoise`;
- the ReSTIR reservoirs and histories (bindings 13–16, 27–35);
- the sampler and bindless specialization constants;
- `IRTRenderPipeline`;
- `samplesPerFrame`, and the global anisotropy and SSS overrides.

### T2: Owen-scrambled Sobol
- `shaders/sampling/sobol.slang` implements Burley 2020's shuffled, scrambled 4-D Sobol. Draws come in groups of
  four, seeded by pixel and group, and each bounce's draws sit in fixed groups.
- The module runs on the CPU through slangc's C++ (`tools/sampling`). `sobol_test` holds it bit for bit to the C++
  reference, holds its stratification to the unscrambled sequence's, and keeps its groups independent.

### T3: the radiance cache
`shaders/rt/radiance_cache.slang`, `radiance_cache_resolve.slang` and `render/rt/radiance_cache.*`:
- **Cells:** 0.05 m × 2^level, with the level set by distance from the camera, keyed by the side a surface faces.
- **Storage:** 32-bit keys with 8 probes; radiance summed ×1024 in integers (`InterlockedAdd`); 2^21 entries
  (72 MiB).
- **Update:** a sparse trace, `cacheUpdate`, takes one pixel in each 5×5 block. It runs 3 bounces and pushes every
  later contribution back into the last 4 vertices' cells.
- **Resolve:** a pass blends (256 samples at most) and evicts (64 frames unseen).
- **Use:** a realtime path ends in a cell past its first vertex when the segment is a voxel long and its lobe spreads
  over the voxel. The query is jittered within the voxel (Binder 2019).
- `resetAccumulation` empties the cache, so captures repeat.

### T4: DLSS Ray Reconstruction and one display path
- `tools/fetch_dlss.py` fetches pinned DLSS SDK files (v310.9.16, SHA-256 each) into the git-ignored `external/DLSS`.
  The build links the static-CRT NGX loader on Windows.
- `VulkanRenderer::rtDefaults` gives a realtime window DLSS RR on NVIDIA and NRD elsewhere. Captures keep `denoise:
  none`.
- **NRD's cinematic post is deleted** (bloom, a tonemap retuned seven times, vignette, a DoF focused at the world
  origin). So is **DLSS's cinematic grade** (contrast, split tone, grain, chromatic aberration).
- `nrd_compose` and the DLSS tonemap now write the frame's output with the tracer's curve, so a window presents what
  a capture reads.
- **Under NRD:** the camera ray's emission has a guide of its own (binding 13), and the first hit's direct light is
  split by lobe (`evalLobes`).

### T6: real time at 1080p
- **Per-stage timers.** `pt.render` is split into `pt.cache.update` (with the frame's setup),
  `pt.cache.resolve`, `pt.trace` and `pt.denoise` (a denoiser and the display transform).
  `render_bench` gained `helmet_rt_1080p`, `play_rt_1080p` (the trace alone) and `play_rt_dlss_1080p`
  (the whole frame under DLSS RR), with budgets proposed and pending.
- **The environment's CDF is in device-local memory** (`render/rt/env_cdf.hpp`): one buffer, the
  marginal then each row's conditional (binding 17; binding 18 deleted), summed in double precision
  and uploaded through the map's staging buffer. A draw returns the density of the texel it drew,
  so NEE no longer evaluates `envPdf` after `sampleEnv`.
- **DLSS RR upscales.** The default preset is Quality (traced at 2/3 of the output a side);
  `OHAO_DLSS_QUALITY=dlaa` keeps native. The jitter sequence has 8 x the area ratio phases, 16 at
  least (NVIDIA's guide).
- **Measured and not kept** (each against the same build, 3 runs):
  - ray-cone texture LOD: sampling every texture at LOD 6 instead of 0 changed nothing measurable
    (the helmet 15.9–17.3 ms against 16.6–17.5);
  - shader execution reordering (`VK_NV_ray_tracing_invocation_reorder`, hinted by instance): 5%
    (the cache update 18%), not worth a second pipeline for GPUs without it; worth measuring again
    once shading is heavier (F4);
  - the shader binding table in device-local memory: no change (the driver caches it);
  - an alias table for the environment (Walker 1977, Vose 1991): O(1), 1.1 ms faster a 1080p frame
    than the CDF's two binary searches, but 14% more error at 16 samples against a 1024-sample render
    (RMSE 18.8 against 16.5 levels; the means agree to 0.02% in linear radiance, so both are unbiased).
    An alias table sends neighbouring Sobol points to unrelated texels; inverting a CDF is a
    continuous warp that keeps them stratified.

### T5: close
- **Deleted:** the SVGF denoiser (`atrous_denoise.*`, `rt_svgf_*.comp`, `DenoiseMode::Atrous`, the API's `atrous`)
  and NRD's "Legacy 4.B" raw-instance branch (OhaoNRDIntegration is always linked with NRD).
- **Docs:** AGENTS.md, README (Third-party SDKs, denoiser table), the CLI guide and the monograph.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **A `--denoise` override that never reached the tracer.** It got there only for NRD, SVGF and DLSS, so
  `--mode rt_realtime --denoise none` rendered one fresh sample a frame (found by the realtime furnace).
- **Zero motion vectors for fresh-sample denoisers.** They were written only while the trace accumulated.
- **The Sobol direction numbers for dimensions 2 and 3 were not Joe-Kuo's.** The GLSL sampler shipped them, and the
  tests that checked them computed their expectations from the same table. They are regenerated by the Bratley-Fox
  recurrence and tested against Joe-Kuo's published (s, a, m).
- **The accumulation images lacked `TRANSFER_SRC`.** Every `--hdr` capture of a path-traced frame made 3 validation
  errors, and the referees never ran under the layer.
- **NRD shown undenoised.** A window presented the undenoised trace under NRD, because NRD's result went to a side
  readback. That result had four further defects:
  - black lights: the camera ray's emission was demodulated by an albedo of 0;
  - specular colour applied twice;
  - the first hit's direct light all in the diffuse channel;
  - depth of field everywhere.
- **The DLSS tonemap reused one descriptor set every frame** (VUID 03047).
- **NGX logged to stdout,** which `ohao serve` reserves for the protocol.
- **`OHAO_DLSS_ENABLED` was a private define** while `PathTracer`'s layout depends on it. `engine_tests` built a
  smaller `PathTracer` on its stack and crashed (an ODR violation). The define is public.

- **T6: the environment's CDF lived in host memory.** Both CDF buffers were `HOST_VISIBLE`, which on
  this laptop is system memory: every step of the two binary searches read over PCIe. Environment
  sampling was 85% of a realtime frame (the helmet room 50 ms with it, 6.5 ms without).
- **T6: DLSS RR's upscaling was never wired.** The feature was created with the output's size equal
  to the render size, the preset was never passed (always DLAA), the evaluation was not given the
  output size and the display transform covered the render size: `OHAO_DLSS_QUALITY=quality` filled
  the top-left 1280x720 of a 1080p frame.
- **T6: `render_bench` rendered the play scene without its level** outside the vfx scenes (the level
  and physics systems were installed only for those). The new realtime play scenes install them
  (`SceneSpec::level`); without them the camera sits inside a closed mesh and the frame is black at
  34 ms. `play_hud_1080p` and `play_anim_1080p` still measure the empty blockout, as their budgets were set.

## 4. Spec corrections

- **SHaRC is built as our own code.** RTXGI's licence bars distributing its source in an MIT repository (spec 4a).
- **The DLSS SDK is fetched, never committed** (spec 4a).
- **`render.settings rt.cache` and the cache's occupancy in `render.stats` are not built.** They move to T6 with the
  per-pass timers.

## 5. Results

**The referee:**
- **Furnace:** the realtime profile matches the reference (0.9992–1.0004).
- **Closed box (Le 1, ρ 0.5):** the reference gives 2.0000 with every bounce, 1.7500 at `--bounces 1` and 1.5000 at
  `--bounces 0`. The realtime profile with the cache gives 1.9720 at 256 frames (×0.986), where 2 bounces alone gave
  1.875. Two realtime runs are bit-identical.
- **Realtime Cornell at 256 frames:** within 0.3% of the offline reference overall; the worst 4×3 cell is 1.4% off.
- `check_material` passes 21 of 21. `check_validation` passes 19 of 19, including DLSS RR on Cornell and the play
  scene.

**Sampler (T2), per-pixel RMSE against closed forms at 64 samples a pixel:**

| Scene | Sobol | PCG |
|---|---|---|
| Closed box | 0.065 | 0.104 |
| Furnace | 0.0112 | 0.0322 |

The furnace's error falls as N^-0.76 under Sobol and N^-0.50 under PCG.

**T6, real time at 1080p** (`render_bench`, medians of 100 frames; the trace is `pt.cache.*` + `pt.trace`):

| Scene at 1080p | Before T6 | After T6 |
|---|---|---|
| Helmet room, trace alone | 44 ms | 16.3 ms |
| Play scene, trace alone | 58 ms | 15.7 ms |
| Play scene, whole frame under DLSS RR | 21.5 ms (native) | 11.1 ms (Quality: 6.6 trace + 0.8 cache + 3.6 DLSS) |

Budgets proposed for Frank (pending, `gatedFrom` 16): the two traces 18 ms, the DLSS frame 12 ms. The
helmet golden moved by isolated pixels (max_abs 5 on 0.27%); every other golden is unchanged. The new
estimator's linear mean at 16 samples is the 1024-sample mean within 0.02%.

**Cost before T6 (one frame's GPU time):**

| Scene | Realtime | Offline |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell, 640×360 | 1.20 ms | 3.62 ms |
| Cornell, 1080p | 7.6 ms | 29.5 ms |
| Helmet room, 1080p | 44 ms | 83 ms |
| Play scene, 1080p | 58 ms | 80 ms |

Direct light alone on the helmet room costs 44 ms. Sobol is about 10% of it (PCG measured 39.9 ms).

**Goldens:** 32 of 32 at each commit. The realtime pair moved three times (T1: the reference's shading with no
spatial filter; T2: Sobol; T3: the cache's bounces) and the eight path-traced goldens moved at T2. Each was looked at,
with notes in the manifest.

## 6. Known gaps

- **The trace alone is not 60 fps at native 1080p** (16 ms); DLSS RR's Quality preset makes the frame 11 ms. Two
  shadow rays a vertex (a light and the environment) are now the largest cost: one RIS-chosen sample is F4's ReSTIR DI.
- **NRD has no upscaling:** a non-NVIDIA realtime frame traces at full size (NRD 4–10 ms on top).
- **`render.settings rt.cache` and the cache's occupancy in `render.stats` are not built.**
- **`render_bench`'s `play_hud_1080p` and `play_anim_1080p` render the play scene without its level**, as their
  budgets were set.
- **The monograph (`site/`) is frozen during the frontier work** (Frank, 2026-10-30): its citation test fails on
  the code this step changed.
- **NRD's specular stays speckled on glossy spheres at 16 frames.** DLSS RR is clean on the same input.
- **`damaged_helmet` / `cli_damaged_helmet` fail in about 2 of 5 runs** by 2–4 levels on 23–38 pixels. This is the
  driver's per-process BVH difference, now beyond the manifest's documented floor of 1 LSB because the reference
  tracer tests a hit against its triangle's geometric normal. The tolerance is not widened: Frank's ruling.
- **The radiance cache has no SH encoding,** so glossy reflections keep tracing instead.
- **A cache cell's 32-bit key can collide,** merging two cells (rare at 2^21).
- **`nrd_frontend.glsl`** keeps only the unpack that `nrd_compose` uses. It goes in F6 (Slang).
