# Sub-project 11, step 3: the schedule and the player

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-28-audio-design.md` ("The step: the schedule", "The frame: the
player", "Performance budgets", "Determinism", "As built (step 3)"); plan
`docs/plans/2026-10-28-audio.md`, Task 3.
**Status:** built (step 3 of 4). Every sound the game plays is now decided in the step by the voice
schedule and played in a window by the `AudioPlayer`; `Footsteps`, `CombatSounds` and `game_audio`
are deleted. Agents cannot read the schedule yet (`audio.info`, `audio.mix` are Task 4). Row 11
stays open.

## 1. Summary

- **The voice schedule decides every sound in the step.** `audio_schedule`, the step's last
  simulate system, turns the step's physics, combat and animation events into commands: `play`
  (with a voice id, variant, occlusion and outcome), `stop` (a stolen voice), `bus` (ducks),
  `snapshot` (from the round), `zone` (with its bed), `occlusion` (re-casts) and `stop_world` (a
  restart or a restore).
- **Priority and stealing** over each bus's cap, its ancestors' and the bank's `voices`: the lowest
  priority goes, then the quietest at the listener, then the oldest; with nothing lower the new
  sound is `dropped`. A voice holds its bank-declared `holdMs` (OPEN 13).
- **The schedule's state rides the World's snapshot**, so a restore, a round restart and a replay
  resume the same schedule; `world.hash` never sees it.
- **The player** plays the schedule one step after its step (OPEN 7) on a clock anchored to the
  device; it replaces the window's two sound players.
- **Enemies step from their clips** (`foot_l`/`foot_r`, OPEN 15); the player keeps physics'
  footsteps.
- **Ambience beds** (generated, OPEN 19): wind outside, room tone inside, looped on the zone.
- **Budgets** for the schedule, the 16-enemy worst case and the player's frame, all far under.

No render golden moved, no `world.hash` moved, and every combat, physics and animation expectation
is unchanged.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **A restart left the device in the `dead` snapshot.** The schedule's state is the round start's
  after a restart, so its snapshot is already `default` and it wrote no change; the device was still
  in `dead`. The first fix (write the transition) made `t28_replay` fail: the replay wrote `default
  over 30` where the recording wrote `default over 0`. Fixed by separating what the device heard
  (not snapshotted) from the schedule's state: after a restore the step first writes `resync`
  commands toward the restored state, then exactly what a recording wrote; a player ignores a zone or
  snapshot it is already in. Tests compare schedules without `resync` lines.
- **`t29_preloaded` counted the bank's own decodes as plays' file opens**: the world's bank loads
  after the schedule's first step, so the test steps once before it takes its count.
- **The 16-enemy bench never reached the ray cap** at first (0 capped in 220 steps): bursts did not
  coincide. The AI measures its attack range from its eye to the player's chest; giving each enemy
  a range 0.6 m short of that distance makes them run in together and fire on the same steps (14
  rounds in one step, 8 rays capped). The bench fails if nothing is capped.
