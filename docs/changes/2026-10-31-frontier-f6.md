# Frontier F6: Slang everywhere

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md` (section 3, step F6: the G-buffer, TAA, bloom, the gizmo
and the remaining compute in Slang; retires every GLSL stage and `layout.glsl`).
**Plan:** `docs/plans/2026-10-31-frontier-f6.md`.
**Status:** built. The frontier renderer's six steps are done.

## 1. Summary

**Every shader is Slang; there is no GLSL and no glslc.** The last thirteen GLSL files (the G-buffer, the post stack's
fullscreen triangle, bloom's two filters, TAA's resolve, the CSM vertex shader, the gizmo, and the generated
`layout.glsl`) are translated line for line, expressions in the same order, and every render golden is unchanged:
33/33 exact, the realtime deferred scenes at max_abs 0. The G-buffer's vertex transform is now one Slang function that
both geometry paths call, so the meshlet path's images equal the instance path's by construction rather than by a
hand-kept copy.

## 2. What was built

- **F6a, the post stack:** `postprocess/fullscreen.slang`, `bloom_downsample.slang`, `bloom_upsample.slang`,
  `taa_resolve.slang`.
- **F6b, CSM and the gizmo:** `shadow/shadow_csm.slang` (the two matrices multiplied first, as GLSL's
  `viewProj * world * p` evaluated), `overlay/gizmo.slang` (two entries: `vertexMain`, `fragmentMain`);
  `includes/scene/scene_data.slang`, the instance table's struct.
- **F6c, the G-buffer:** `core/gbuffer.slang`: `vertexMain` (also the transparent pass's meshes), `opaque`
  (`[earlydepthstencil]`) and `masked` (the alpha cutoff's discard); `includes/scene/gbuffer_vertex.slang`:
  `GVertex`, `instanceXform` and `transformVertex`, which `core/gbuffer.slang` and `cull/meshlets.slang` both call.
- **F6d, GLSL retired:** `shaders/CMakeLists.txt` has no glslc rule; `tools/gen_layout.py` writes `layout.slang` and
  `ohao/gpu/shared_layout.hpp` only; `ohao doctor` lists no `tools.glslc` and follows Slang imports only (its sources
  are `.slang` files with entry points); `check_layout`'s compile test and `check_doctor`'s fake shader trees are Slang.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- None in the translation. Two Slang details the translation depends on: a vertex shader that reads firstInstance or
  firstVertex needs `SV_VulkanInstanceID`/`SV_VulkanVertexID` (HLSL's `SV_InstanceID` leaves the base out, and the
  G-buffer's and CSM's firstInstance is the draw's index); and a file with several entry points names its SPIR-V
  `<flattened>.slang.<entry>.spv` (`core_gbuffer.slang.opaque.spv`).

## 4. Results

- Render goldens 33/33 exact after each of F6a, F6b and F6c (no re-capture); `cull_parity_gpu_test` 49/49 (meshlets =
  instances = the fallback), `light_cluster_gpu_test` 22/22 and `light_emitter_gpu_test` 31/31 (the CSM fallback),
  `vfx_gpu_test` 44/44 (forward parity through the shared vertex shader), `engine_tests` 84/84; `ohao_game` under
  validation for 300 frames with a resize: 0 messages through teardown; `check_doctor` 11/11, `check_layout` 5/5;
  `tools/check.py` 58/58 after F6a-c and after F6d.
- slangc lowers `discard` to `OpDemoteToHelperInvocation`, as glslc did (the Vulkan 1.3 feature stays on).

## 5. Known gaps

- Build directories made before F6 keep the old GLSL SPIR-V (`*.vert.spv`, `*.frag.spv`) beside the new; nothing
  loads them, and a fresh build has none.
