## 1. Summary

- **Smoke proxies reach the RT traces.** A smoke record publishes closed-form density spheres
  (`ohao/vfx/smoke_math.hpp`, the plan's proxy math, at most `caps.proxies`). They are uploaded each frame as a
  byte buffer in set 1, binding 7 of the alpha tables (`ohao/render/rt/rt_alpha_tables.*`), and
  `shaders/rt/smoke_proxy.slang` gives `smokeTransmittance` over a segment. `shadow_trace` lights a sample with
  probability `T`; `gi_trace` multiplies its radiance by `T` and adds 0.12 in-scatter by `1 − T`.
- **`render.settings vfx.smokeInGi`** switches both (on by default).
- **The grenade.** The `explode` binding (`ohao/ecs/vfx.cpp`, `onExplode`) writes the boom and smoke effects and
  the scorch decal, and `grenade_boom`'s `light` becomes a transient light through the clustered lights
  (`flashLights` in `ecs/vfx_draw.cpp`, `RenderScene::setTransientLights`). A grenade in flight is drawn as a
  presented sphere between its last two steps (`CombatSystem::grenadesInFlight`, `RenderScene::setPresentedSpheres`);
  it is never in the registry.
- **The level hint** `vfx` makes a `VfxEmitter` on a node (`ohao/level/level_hints.*`, `tests/level/extras_schema.json`).
- **Agent surface.** `render.settings vfx {particles, soft, tracers, decals, smokeInGi}`: presentation switches,
  all on by default, never in the World. `render.stats vfx {particles, objectsSorted, decalsDrawn, tracers,
  proxies, particlesSortedMax}` (absent without a deferred renderer). `vfx.info`'s `particles` is the newest
  frame's lit slots (null headless).

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **RT GI never ran in the doorway test (test 34's first failure).** `vfx_gpu_test`'s `capture()` installed the
  VFX system but not the CLI context's levels and physics. The doorway's `Level` never expanded: the scene had no
  instances, `scene.upload` built no TLAS (`RenderScene::recordTlas` returns at `count == 0`), so `rt.shadows` and
  `rt.gi` were absent from the frame's passes and the frame was the sky (both captures read 54.67). The render
  settings were right; `rtGIWanted` and `rtGIAvailable` were both true. Fixed in the test (`level::installLevels`,
  `ecs::installPhysics`, as `tools/ohao/main.cpp`). Recorded in `AGENTS.md` as a gotcha.
- **Doorway measurement and the blockout's geometry (not a defect).** The GI path responds to smoke (section 5:
  a dense cloud over the floor, and a cloud inside the room at z = 1.8). The doorway cloud at z = 3.1 barely moves
  the patch because the blockout room is open to the sky. This is a question about the spec's expectation, not
  an engine fault (section 6, Ruling A).

## 4. Spec corrections and deviations

- **`soft` is a switch.** It zeroes the emitter's `motion.w`, which the shader reads as "soft fade off".
- **`vfx.info` `particles` is a number** (the newest frame's lit slots), not the spec's object; null headless.
- **`render.settings` golden:** `native.jsonl`'s `render.settings` read-back now carries `vfx`
  (the new field in the reply). Reason: the group is part of the settings' shape.
- **Serve goldens' `schemaHash` moved** (9 sessions: `animation`, `audio`, `combat`, `errors`, `level`, `mcp`,
  `native`, `physics`, `player`). Reason: `VfxSettings` is a new registered type, and `render.stats` and
  `render.settings` method docs changed. `golden_diff_check` (in `build/sp12/`) confirmed each diff is the hash
  alone, except `native`'s new `vfx` group above.
- **The test grenade's sphere** is presentation only (`kGrenadeRadius`, 0.05 m). Frank's ruling 26 (drawn: yes) is
  taken as recommended; the look shot is not taken yet.
