# The referee: linear HDR capture, the closed form, and two path tracer light bugs

**Spec:** none (the renderer plan's "the path tracer as referee", Frank 2026-10-29).
**Status:** built. Gate 51 of 51. Goldens 32 of 32, one moved (`cli_anim_rig_pt`, looked at).

## 1. Summary

To judge the deferred frame by a number, it is compared with the path tracer, the engine's ground truth for light transport.
Three things were needed and one was found on the way:

1. **Linear HDR capture.** The two renderers tonemap differently (deferred: `tonemapping.frag` at the capture's exposure,
   0.6 by default; the path tracer: ACES at a fixed 0.5 in its ray-gen), so their PNGs cannot be compared. `render.capture
   {hdr}` and `ohao run --hdr` write the frame's linear radiance as a PFM.
2. **The closed form.** `check_analytic.py`: a white floor under one light, nothing else, against `albedo/π × irradiance`.
3. **The comparison.** `check_parity.py`: six reference scenes rendered both ways, deferred's distance from the path tracer in
   stops.
4. **The path tracer was wrong**, so the referee needed refereeing first: it lit a sun 7.8× too bright and every point or spot
   light under 5.6 cm too dark (3% of its light at 1 cm). Fixed; both renderers now hold the closed form within 2.4%.

## 2. What was built

- **`VulkanRenderer::readbackLinear()`** (`render_dispatch.cpp`): after the frame, the lit image (deferred, RGBA16F: lighting,
  sky, transparents, SSR, before bloom, TAA, exposure and tonemapping) or the path tracer's accumulation (RGBA32F, before any
  denoiser) as RGBA32F. `readImageRGBA` is the old OIDN readback lambda made a member, for both formats and any layout;
  `readbackHDRBuffers` uses it.
- **`runtime::writePfm`**; `RenderRequest::hdrPath`; `render.capture`'s `hdr` (sandboxed like `path`; a capture may write only the
  PFM); `ohao run --hdr`. A realtime window refuses `hdr` (`E_COMMAND`: its frame is captured as presented); a lockstep window
  captures it.
- **`tests/renderer/check_analytic.py`** (CTest, gpu): sun at 90° and 45°, point lights of radius 1 cm and 20 cm, a spot pointing
  down; deferred (32 frames) and path traced (256 samples, no denoiser); each within 5% of the closed form.
- **`tests/renderer/check_parity.py`** (CTest, slow; a report, exit 0 unless a capture fails): `stops` (mean |log₂(deferred/PT)|
  over 8×8 blocks), `ratio`, a 4×4 grid, `build/parity/<scene>.error.png`.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

**The HDR capture is exact.** Put through each renderer's tonemap, the PFM is the PNG of the same capture to within 1 LSB on
every channel, deferred (`ACES(0.6 x)^(1/2.2)`) and path traced (`ACES(0.5 x)^(1/2.2)`), Cornell at 640×360.

**The path tracer's lights** (`includes/rt/pt_lights.glsl`, `ptEmitterArea`, and its three ray-gen shaders, ten sites): every
light's emitted radiance was `I / max(area, 0.01)`:

- A **directional light** has no emitter, but was divided by 4πr² of its `radius` (0.1 m by default: ÷0.126). It is now 1: the
  intensity is the irradiance on a surface facing the light, as in the deferred frame.
- The **0.01 m² floor** dimmed every point or spot light under 5.6 cm, while the camera saw its emitter mesh at the unfloored
  `I/(πr²)`. The radius is already clamped to 1 cm, so the floor is gone.

| Case (closed form) | Deferred | Path tracer before | Path tracer after |
|---|---|---|---|
| sun 90° (0.3183) | 0.992 | 7.80 | **0.980** |
| sun 45° (0.2251) | 1.004 | 7.83 | **0.983** |
| point, r 1 cm (0.3979) | 0.993 | 0.031 | **0.979** |
| point, r 5 cm | 0.993 | 0.769 | **0.979** |
| point, r 20 cm | 0.993 | 0.979 | **0.979** |
| spot, down | 0.993 | | **0.979** |

What stays inside the 5%: deferred's Burley diffuse, the path tracer's (1 − F) on its diffuse lobe.

## 4. Results

**The referee** (`check_parity.py`, 640×360; deferred/PT mean luminance and the mean error in stops):

| Scene | Before the light fix | After |
|---|---|---|
| Cornell | 0.450, 2.80 stops | 0.450, 2.80 |
| Helmet room | 0.454, 2.28 | 0.454, 2.28 |
| Metal spheres | 0.392, 2.39 | 0.392, 2.39 |
| Area light | 0.721, 0.76 | 0.721, 0.76 |
| Sponza (sun) | 0.499, 1.28 | **0.620, 0.96** |
| Blockout room (sun) | 0.107, 2.81 | **0.458, 1.16** |

With direct light held to the closed form in both, what is left is indirect light: the closed rooms (Cornell, the helmet and
sphere rooms) are lit mostly by light that has bounced more than once, and deferred's RT GI traces one bounce. Cornell's grid puts
the worst of it in the corners (deferred at 3–12% of the path tracer there).

**Goldens:** `cli_anim_rig_pt` moved (its sun): before, blown out to white; after, the rig's tan and the floor read as the deferred
frame's do. Every other golden is exact (their point lights are at least 5.6 cm; their suns are deferred). **Schema:**
`render.capture`'s `hdr` moves `schemaHash` (0391a82a80d4d282 → 0379a711fb1e849e) in the CLI and serve expectations, nothing else
in them; `tools/list` is 25,257 bytes (cap 28 KB).

**Gate:** 51 of 51 (`python tools/check.py`, labels quick, gpu, docs), `check_analytic` among them; `check_parity` is
`slow` (the table above is its run).

## 5. Known gaps

- **Multi-bounce indirect light** (the referee's remaining gap): the next step.
- **A scene without an environment** differs between the renderers: deferred lights it with the constant ambient (0.35) and draws
  its procedural sky; the path tracer has neither (black sky, no ambient). The anim rig shows it. A ruling for Frank.
- **Exposure:** the path tracer tonemaps at a fixed 0.5, deferred at the capture's exposure (0.6 by default). The referee compares
  linear radiance, so it does not matter there; for pictures it does.
