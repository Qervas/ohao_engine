# Sub-project 11, step 1: the mixer graph

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-28-audio-design.md` ("Data: the bank and the mix", "Performance
budgets", "As built (step 1)"); plan `docs/plans/2026-10-28-audio.md`, Task 1.
**Status:** built (step 1 of 4). The game still plays through the category API, which now maps
onto the default bank's buses, so it sounds as before. Row 11 stays open.

## 1. Summary

The audio system now has a mixer with buses, read from a bank:
- **The bank:** one `.audio.json` per game, named by a new saved singleton `AudioMix`. It declares
  buses as a tree with voice caps and a return bus, sounds, distance shapes, ducks, snapshots and
  reverb presets. The parser reports every error at its JSON pointer.
- **The buses:** each is an `ma_sound_group` feeding a node of ours with a gain linear in dB, a
  low-pass ramped in log frequency, a mute/solo gate and a send.
- **Bank voices:** they play decoded PCM, start exactly on the frame asked for, and fade out
  themselves when stopped.
- **Rendering without a device:** test 26 and `audio_bench` render the mixer this way and listen
  to the result.

The play scene names `assets/audio/slice.audio.json`, which carries Feel's values. Step 3's voice
schedule will be the first to play through it. No simulation result changed. The expectations
that moved are hashes only, for the new saved type's name, plus the play scene's snapshot growing
by its new `AudioMix`.

## 2. What was built

- `ohao/audio/audio_bank.{hpp,cpp}`:
  - `parseAudioBank` (simdjson);
  - `defaultBank()` (master over `sfx` 24, `music`, `ambient`);
  - `resolveSoundFiles` (`{n}` variants, the first folder holding any; `assets/local/audio` first
    in the slice);
  - `audioBankJsonSchema()`, exported by `ohao schema audio-bank [-o file]`.
- `ohao/audio/audio_system.{hpp,cpp}`:
  - `loadBank(bank, root)` decodes every variant (f32, at the engine's rate) and rebuilds the
    buses.
  - `play(sound, variant, placement, startFrame)` and `stop(handle, fadeFrames)`.
  - `setBus(bus, db, lowPassHz, rampFrames)`, `setMute`, `setSolo` and `setBusSend`.
  - `engineFrame()`, and `render(out, frames)` on a `noDevice` engine.
  - `AudioBusNode`: per frame, the dB ramp, the biquad (coefficients every 16 frames), the 2 ms
    gate, and a second output as the send.
  - `AudioVoiceSource`: silence until `startFrame`, then the data, then a stop's linear fade.
- The category API: a category volume is its bus's group volume; master is the root's. A voice's
  own volume is its base volume times the occlusion cut. Bank voices sit outside Feel's
  24-voice cap.
- `AudioMix { asset }` in `ecs/components.hpp`: registered and documented, with its writer,
  `scene_tests` coverage and the corpus case `22_audio_mix`. It sits on the play scene's
  `Controls` entity.
- `assets/audio/slice.audio.json`:
  - **Buses:** the spec's (master, world, weapons 12, impacts 8, foley 10, ambience 4, voice 2,
    ui 4, music 2, reverb return; 32 voices).
  - **Shapes:** Feel's (gunfire 1–60 m at rolloff 1; near 0.5–20 m at rolloff 1.5).
  - **Volumes:** Feel's.
  - **Ducks and snapshots:** the spec's proposals (OPEN rulings 8, 9).
  - **Reverb:** three presets without beds.
- `tools/bench/audio_bench.cpp` and `tests/bench/audio_budgets.json`, registered as
  `LABEL bench SERIAL`.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **miniaudio's start time is wrong inside a block.** Two clicks scheduled for frames 1000 and 1800
  landed at 1444 and 1924. `ma_node_read_pcm_frames` (0.11.21) offsets a start inside a block by
  (block end − start) instead of (start − block begin). Fixed by giving bank voices their own data
  source, which reads `startFrame − engineFrame()` frames of silence.
- **`ma_sound_stop_with_fade_in_pcm_frames` cut at once:** a 0.5 step in one sample. The voice's
  source now fades over the frames asked for (largest step 0.0052 over 96 frames).
- **The pitch resampler delayed every voice by one frame per node.** After the source fix, onsets
  landed at 1004/1804, then at 1003/1803 with pitch off on the voice; the rest came from three
  groups. Voices and bus groups are now made with `MA_SOUND_FLAG_NO_PITCH` (nothing sets pitch),
  and onsets land at 1000/1800.
- **A test flaw, not a code bug.** `t26_snapshot_blend`'s spectrum case compared two different
  windows of noise at one Goertzel bin and read −17 dB for an expected −9. It now compares the same
  noise frames with and without the snapshot, over nine bins (−8.92 dB).
- **`ohao fmt` was not used on the play scene.** It reflowed the hand-written file and dropped its
  empty `Transform`s, so the `AudioMix` was added as two lines by hand.

## 4. Spec corrections

Recorded in the spec's "As built (step 1)":
- The bus node is ours instead of `ma_lpf_node` plus a splitter.
- Start and stop belong to the voice's source.
- Pitch is off on voices and groups.
- Banks are decoded once at load.
- The interfaces as built: `Result` errors, `loadBank(bank, root)`, `SoundHandle`.
- The slice names footsteps per surface.
- Reverb beds wait for step 2.

`holdMs` follows the spec's proposals (enemy fire 600, footsteps 250).

## 6. Known gaps

- Nothing plays through the bank in the game yet. `runtime/footsteps` and
  `runtime/combat_sounds` use the category API until step 3 replaces them.
- The reverb return is a pass-through bus (step 2).
- A solo or mute set the frame a sound starts lets 2 ms of it through (the gate's ramp).
- With a device, `startFrame` is measured against the engine clock read on the calling thread.
  The schedule's horizon (step 3) is what makes it exact in a window.
- **Pacing (SP9 ruling 21, still open).** A `feel_bench` rerun after the gate reproduced the miss:

  | Mode | Look latency median | p99/median | Stalls in the minute |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | immediate | 5.54 ms | 8.64 | 36 |
  | mailbox | 5.06 ms | 2.21 | 29 |
  | fifo | (frame time median 260.6 ms) | — | 149 |

  At the animation close the stalls were 21, 1 and 143. This step cannot be the cause:
  `feel_bench`'s window host is made without `LiveConfig::footsteps`, so it creates no
  `AudioSystem`. The only difference in its run is the play scene's `AudioMix`, which no system
  reads. The stalls are the presentation engine holding the swapchain (AGENTS.md, "Stalls"), more
  of them today than at the animation close.
