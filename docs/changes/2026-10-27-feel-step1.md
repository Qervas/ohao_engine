# Feel, step 1: measure the latency and the pacing

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-27-feel-design.md` ("Latency: the definition", "Present mode and late
input sampling", "Frame pacing", "The agent-facing surface", "Performance budgets", "As built (step
1)", rulings 15–18)
**Status:** done 2026-10-27 (plan `docs/plans/2026-10-27-feel.md`, Task 1); nothing drawn changes

## 1. Summary

The engine now measures how long a mouse movement takes to reach the screen and how evenly frames
come, and reports both. A window presents in the mode asked for (immediate, mailbox, fifo), every
present carries its frame number, and a thread asks the presentation engine when each was shown
(`VK_KHR_present_wait`). A realtime window stamps each frame (poll, view, submit, present) and the
look movements it shows; `render.stats` reports `latency` and `pacing`, `ohao_game --latency` draws
them, and `feel_bench` turns a scripted minute per present mode into a baseline. On this machine (RTX
5070 Laptop, 240 Hz display) a movement takes **32.1 ms** (median, immediate present) to reach the
screen: about 8 waiting for a step, 17 for the step the interpolation draws behind, 6 in frames
queued ahead. The 15 ms budget is pending until step 2, which is built to remove the first two.

## 2. What was built

- `gpu/vulkan/present_target.hpp`: `PresentMode {Fifo, Mailbox, Immediate}`, `presentModeName`,
  `presentModeFromName`; `PresentTarget::mode` replaces `vsync`.
- `Swapchain`: `choosePresentMode(modes, want)` (immediate → mailbox → fifo; mailbox never falls to
  immediate), the mode given logged once, `present(queue, image, presentId)`, `hostMutex()` (held by
  present, rebuild and destroy), `generation()`, `presentMode()`.
- Device setup: `VK_KHR_present_id` and `VK_KHR_present_wait` with their feature bits when the device
  has both and there is a surface; `vkWaitForPresentKHR` resolved from the device.
- `gpu/vulkan/present_waiter.{hpp,cpp}`: `PresentWaiter`, a thread polling `vkWaitForPresentKHR`
  with a zero timeout every ~0.25 ms (a high-resolution waitable timer) under the swapchain's
  mutex; a present unconfirmed after 250 ms, or without the extensions, keeps its call's return.
  `VulkanRenderer::presentFrame`, `takePresentStamps()`, `presentWaitEnabled()`, `presentMode()`,
  `lastSubmitTime()`.
- `runtime/frame_log.{hpp,cpp}`: `FrameStamps`, `LookSample`, `FrameLog` (a ring, 1,024 frames by
  default), `latency(frames)`, `pacing(frames)`, `percentile`.
- `LiveHost`: per-frame stamps, look movements from the window's polls and from `input.inject`

- `render.stats`: `latency {frames, samples, medianMs, p95Ms, source, present, stages}` and `pacing
  {frames, frameMs {median, p95, p99}, steps, discardedSteps}`, realtime windows only.
- `ohao_game --present MODE`, `--latency` (the readout top left); `ohao serve --window --present
  MODE`; `--vsync` is `--present fifo`. The HUD font gained `.`.
- `Window::refreshRate()`.
- `tools/bench/feel_bench.cpp` (label `bench`, GPU, serial) and `tests/bench/feel_budgets.json`
  (`look_latency_ms` 15 and `frame_p99_over_median` 1.5, gated from step 2).
- Tests: `runtime_tests` `sp9_t20_present_mode_choice` (replacing `choose_present_mode`),
  `sp9_t19_latency_stages_ordered`, `sp9_t20_pacing_stats`; `check_live.py` sp9 tests 19, 20 (a

- Docs: AGENTS.md (the suites, `feel_bench`, frame timing), ARCHITECTURE.md (presents and the frame

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **The bench's first mouse script phase-locked to the step clock.** A movement every 50 ms is
  three 1/60 s steps, so every movement met the step clock at the same phase: poll → consume read
  15.0 ms in every mode of the first smoke run. The movements are now 40–60 ms apart on a
  golden-ratio sequence; poll → consume reads 7.9–9.0 ms, about half a step, as it should.
- Tests of mine: the check_live test asked `render.stats` for 600 frames (its limit is 120: the
  call was refused and the test saw nothing); it asks for 120 after the last movements reach a frame.

## 4. Spec corrections

- The fallback present stamp is the present call's return (`present_call`), not a frame's fence
  seen from another thread (that would race the fence's reset) (ruling 15).
- The waiter polls with a zero timeout under the swapchain's mutex rather than blocking in
  `vkWaitForPresentKHR` (ruling 16); the plan's "polled once a frame" would have stamped to a frame.
- `FrameLog`, not `FrameTiming` (`render::FrameTiming` already names the GPU pass timings).
- `render.stats`' `latency` also names the window's present mode (`present`).

## 6. Known gaps

- The 500–780 ms latency outliers: every mode has a few frames a minute that run 3+ steps (126–248
  steps discarded by the clock's 5-step cap), half-second hitches whose cause is not found yet (step
  2's pacing work).
- fifo settles into two-refresh (8.3 ms) frames after about 20 s of the run.
- `feel_bench` has no noise detection (ruling 17) and opens visible windows (a hidden one may never
  present on other machines; here it did).
- The readout's `LAT` stays `/` until a look movement is shown; a game without mouse motion shows
  only frame times.
