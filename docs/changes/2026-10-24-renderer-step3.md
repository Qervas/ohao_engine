## 1. Summary

The Assimp loader now walks the glTF node tree, so a model arrives in its file's scene coordinates. DamagedHelmet, whose node rotates it upright, stands up in every helmet scene and golden. It used to lie on its back.

- **One glTF loader.** The tinygltf loader, which `env_demo` and `turntable` used, is deleted together with its dependency. Both examples now load through `ModelLoader`.
- **The up axis.** `SceneFramer` guessed the up axis from the bounds, and the upright helmet is deeper than it is tall, so the guess tipped it over again. glTF is Y up by its specification, and the framer now treats it so.
- **The camera distance.** The upright helmet then cut off at the bottom of the deferred frame. The framer now fits a deep model's height by moving the camera back, and both helmet scene files moved their cameras accordingly.

## 2. What was built

- **`ModelLoader::loadAssimp` (`model_loader.cpp`): a depth-first walk from `mRootNode`.**
  - Each node's `mTransformation` is accumulated onto its parent's, and every mesh the node references is emitted in that space into the model's one vertex and index list.
  - Positions take the full matrix.
  - Normals take the inverse transpose of the upper 3x3.
  - Tangents take the 3x3 itself, with handedness negated under a mirror.
  - A negative determinant swaps two indices of each face.
  - A mesh referenced from two nodes is emitted twice.
- **Deleted:**
  - `ohao/scene/asset/model_gltf.cpp` (`Model::loadFromGLTF`);
  - `external/cmake/tinygltf.cmake` and its include and link lines;
  - the `MaterialData` glTF fields only tinygltf wrote (`baseColorFactor`, `metallicFactor`, `roughnessFactor`, four texture paths, `alphaMode`, `alphaCutoff`, `doubleSided`).
- **`env_demo` and `turntable`** load with `ModelLoader::load`. In place of their inline copies of the bounds guess, they ask the framer for the up axis.
- **`SceneFramer`:**
  - `computeFraming(const Model&)` forces Y up for a `.gltf`/`.glb` source.
  - `isYUp(const Model&)` exposes the same rule to hosts that place the model themselves.
  - The camera's z is `max(1.7 S, z_front + 1.1 · h/2 / tan(fov/2))`: at least the old distance, and far enough back that the model's height plus 10% fits the vertical field of view at its nearest face.
  - Every caller passes a `Model`: `model_viewer`, `scene_tests` and `scene_view_gpu_test`.
- **Scene files:**
  - `damaged_helmet.scene.json` was regenerated by `model_viewer --save-scene`. The camera moved from `[0, -6, 25.5]` to `[0, -6.891139, 30.534174]`, and the model's transform is now `position [0.022334216, -6.8912344, 1.6843946]`, scale 9.
  - `helmet_game.scene.json` received the same camera position (it keeps its `FlyController`) and the same model transform.
- **Follow-on outputs:**
  - `tests/cli/expected/summary.out`: the scene bounds' max z is the camera's, 30.534174.
  - The fly session in `docs/agents/ohao-serve.md`: the camera starts at z 30.534174 and ends at 27.53422, still exactly 3 m, and it replays to its own hash.
- **Fixture `tests/assets/node_transforms.gltf`:** one triangle, (0,0,0) (1,0,0) (0,1,0) with normal +Z, referenced three times:
  - by a root rotated 90° about Z and scaled 2;
  - under that root, by a child translated 5 along X;
  - by a second root translated to z 5 and mirrored in X.
- **Test 4 (`scene_tests`):**
  - `sp2b_t04_gltf_node_transforms_reach_vertices_normals_and_winding`: the 9 positions within 1e-6, each normal +Z within 1e-6, and each face's geometric normal agreeing with its vertex normals, so the mirrored copy is wound the right way.
  - `sp2b_t04_damaged_helmet_stands_upright`:
    - the bounds are upright (Y spans ±0.901 and the depth of 2.0 lies along Z);
    - the framer leaves the model unrotated, and `isYUp` says Y;
    - all eight corners of the placed bounds fall inside a 16:9 view from the framed camera. The worst corner is at 0.909 of the half view, against about 1.18 at the old z of 25.5.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

1. **The framer re-tipped the upright helmet.** The bounds guess (Y up when the Y extent ≥ the Z extent) reads the helmet, 1.80 tall and 2.00 deep, as Z up and rotates it −90° about X: the node fix was undone downstream, and the images were unchanged. glTF is now Y up in the framer. `env_demo` and `turntable` carried their own copies of the same guess and would have tipped it too; they now use `isYUp`.
2. **The upright helmet left the frame.** The deferred images cut the chin at the bottom row (helmet pixels on row 1079, x 869–1050). The fixed 1.7 S distance assumes the model is flat at its centre; the helmet's face is 9 units in front of it. The monograph's `camera/scene-framer` unit had predicted this overflow (1.32 of the frame for a model as deep as it is tall). The framer now fits by distance.
3. **Dead material fields.** With tinygltf gone, nothing wrote or read the `MaterialData` glTF fields. They are deleted, and the `path-tracer/hit-miss` unit no longer claims a loader records an alpha mode.
4. **Monograph:**
   - `scene/loaders` described two glTF importers and the tinygltf route's divergences (UVs, normals, base color, roughness), and claimed that no loader walks the node tree. It also claimed that nothing under `tests/` loads a model.
   - `camera/scene-framer` called `forceYUp` dead and the camera distance fixed.
   - `deferred/gbuffer`, `path-tracer/hit-miss` and `systems/build` cited `model_gltf.cpp`.
   - The routing figure had a tinygltf row.
   - All are corrected, together with the loaders page's generator entry, its summary string and the glossary's "via tinygltf".

## 4. Spec corrections

- **Tangents:** they transform by the upper 3x3, not by the inverse transpose; a tangent lies in the surface, so it transforms as a direction. Their handedness is negated under a mirror.
- **Submeshes:** each (node, mesh) pair is emitted into the model's one vertex and index list. Submeshes still come from material runs at upload, not one per (node, mesh).
- **New sections:**
  - **Up axis:** forced Y for glTF, through `isYUp` for the examples.
  - **The camera distance:** fitted for deep models. Both helmet scenes' cameras moved in the code commit, with the code that frames them, so every commit's gates stay green. The golden commit records the move.
- **Review focus 5** (a mirrored node sharing a mesh with an unmirrored one) is covered by test 4's fixture.
- **Frank's ruling (2026-10-24 18:01):**
  - A new section, "Reference quality": `quality: realtime | reference`. Reference means 8 shadow rays per light for every `castShadows` light, and 32 GI rays per pixel at full resolution, with no filter, denoiser or reprojection. A capture accumulates them as a plain running mean.
  - It is reachable through `render.settings`, a capture's `quality` param, `ohao run --quality reference` and the examples' `reference` flag.
  - It is unbudgeted. It gets exact reference goldens (`cornell_box_reference`, `helmet_game_reference`).
  - It is folded into build steps 7 (the shadow half, the switch, the CLI, the goldens), 8 (the GI half) and 15 (`render.settings.quality`), and into tests 9, 10 and 12 and decisions-record row 17. Budgets are unchanged.
