## 1. Summary

The play scene's rifle is now a first-person gun. It is drawn through its own 60° lens, held
inside the player's capsule so it never pokes into a wall, and it sways with a turn, bobs with
the walk and kicks with each round. All of it is computed at render rate from what the frame
draws, and none of it is saved, hashed or snapshotted. It casts no shadow and takes no GI; it is
lit by the world.

A window now also shows combat:
- muzzle bursts, sparks and dust coloured by the surface hit, as GPU particles;
- sounds through one mixer with a 24-voice cap and distance curves per kind;
- an enemy's round or footstep heard through a wall plays 8 dB down and muffled.

The won screen no longer draws the crosshair over "ENTER TO RESTART".

The RT GI speckles at the doorway were traced to the HDRI's sun. The cheap fix is a look change
across every HDRI scene, so it moves to row 16 with the finding written down.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **The gun vanished at `depthScale` 0.25.** The projection is `glm::perspective` without
  `GLM_FORCE_DEPTH_ZERO_TO_ONE`; under Vulkan's [0, 1] depth everything nearer than about twice the
  near plane is clipped (0.0999 m at near 0.05). The drawn pose was right (checked with the
  identity remap, then a depth-only shrink). Fixed by `depthScale` 0.55: the gun spans
  0.108–0.27 m, inside the 0.3 m capsule. The engine-wide depth range is row 16's (§6).
- **The muzzle light stayed at the old gun pose** when the gun moved to its drawn place. Lights now
  take their `RenderPose`, and the emitter sphere scales with the remap.
- **The voice cap would have been per player:** footsteps and combat each opened their own device.
  They now share the host's.
- **The crosshair over the won screen** (SP7 leftover): fixed.

## 6. Known gaps

- **Pacing** (ruling 21) is open: `frame_p99_over_median` was 1.99 in step 2's quiet run and 2.01
  in this step's, against 1.5. Frank's options are in ruling 21.
- **The renderer's depth range** clips below about twice the near plane (above); a
  `[0, 1]`-depth projection (or reversed Z) is row 16 work. With it, `depthScale` can come down.
- **The doorway speckles** are row 16's (above). A fix should take the sun out of the GI rays'
  environment where a directional light stands for it, or sample it as a light.
- **The path-traced `damaged_helmet` golden drifts run to run.** In 4 of 7 renders this step it was
  off by max_abs 2 on 1 px at compare resolution and 1 on another; it is exact in the rest.
  - Two BVH layouts alternate between runs. The documented floor is 1 LSB.
  - It is not this step's camera ray mask: the failing runs had the mask at 7, and so did passing
    runs.
  - Not re-baselined, tolerance unchanged.
- **The particles are the engine's existing presets.** The muzzle burst's emitter sphere and
  flash read as a white disc at the gun's tip; tuning is row 12's (VFX).
