# Frontier F4: direct light, resampled

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md` (section 2, "Direct light"; section 3, step F4).
**Plan:** `docs/plans/2026-10-30-frontier-f4.md`, T1–T4.
**Status:** built; four gated frame budgets missed, a ruling for Frank (section 6).

## 1. Summary

**The deferred frame's direct light is ReSTIR DI over the path tracer's lights.** Every light but flashes and every
emissive triangle is sampled, resampled across pixels and frames, shadowed by one ray to the point kept, shaded with
OpenPBR's exact lobes, and denoised: MegaLights' structure (sample, trace, shade, denoise). RT shadows (8 channels, 4
lights traced a pixel, the rest unshadowed) and the lighting loop's shadowed lights are gone. Any number of lights is
shadowed; area lights and emissive surfaces are lit by points on them, as the path tracer lights them.

**One light model.** The path tracer's light code is now a module both renderers import (`shaders/rt/lights.slang`),
and the emissive triangle table (F3) is the renderer's, recorded by whichever renderer draws a frame.

## 2. What was built

### T1: one light set
- `shaders/rt/lights.slang`: the path tracer's lights (analytic lights, emissive triangles, selection by power) moved
  out of `rt/path_tracer.slang` unchanged in arithmetic (its goldens unchanged); `drawLightPoint` (a point on a light,
  its density in that light's measure) and `arriveFrom` (what a light point gives a surface: direction, distance,
  radiance, geometry term) for resampling.
- `EmissiveLights` belongs to the `VulkanRenderer` (one table, both renderers); its collect pass reads emissive textures
  through the bindless table's own set, and its barriers name the ray tracing stage only where the device has it.

### T2: ReSTIR DI
- `RTDirectTechnique` (`render/rt/rt_direct_technique.*`), `rt/restir_di.slang` (`initial`, `shade`),
  `rt/di_denoise.slang` (`temporal`, `atrous`); passes `di.sample`, `di.shade`, `di.denoise` after `emissive.lights`.
- **di.sample:** each pixel writes a compact surface (position, distance, normal, GGX alpha, diffuse and specular
  reflectance) and draws `rtDirect.candidates` (4) light points kept by RIS against a cheap target (luminance of Le x
  Lambert under GGX x G); last frame's reservoir where last frame's surface is the same (5% distance, 18 degrees), M
  capped at 20x; one visibility ray; the boiling filter.
- **di.shade:** `rtDirect.spatial` (2) neighbours within 30 px, Z normalisation with a visibility ray a neighbour, the
  shading ray, OpenPBR's `evalLobes`, diffuse and specular each over its directional albedo.
- **di.denoise:** a reprojected accumulation (16 frames; a pixel over twice its 3x3 neighbours' maximum clamped first)
  and three 3x3 a-trous steps (1, 2, 4), guided by an 8-byte guide image; specular spreads only as far as the surface
  is rough.
- **Reference quality** reuses nothing and averages the frames since the reset.
- Lighting multiplies the two images by the albedos and leaves every light but flashes out of its loop; RT GI's bounce
  drops what a hit emits when RT direct light runs (`GIInput::emissionIsDirect`).
- `shaders/includes/lighting/gbuffer_material.slang`: the G-buffer's material with the weather, one decode for
  lighting and resampling.

### T3: the retirement
- **Deleted:** `rt/shadow_trace.slang`, `rt/shadow_filter.slang`, `RTShadowTechnique`, `IShadowTechnique`, `ShadowInput`,
  `ShadowOutput`, the shadow channels (`kMaxShadowLights`, `kShadowLightsPerPixel`, `kShadowReferenceRays`,
  `kShadowHistoryFrames`, `kShadowFilterTile`, `kShadowFilterRadius`, `ShadowLight`, `ShadowTracePush`),
  `RenderScene::shadowLights`/`shadowCastersWithoutChannel`, `render.stats shadowLightsDropped`, the lighting pass's
  integer stand-in image.
- `LightGpu.params.w`: 1 casts shadows, 0 not, -1 a flash. Transparent meshes and particles take the first casting
  directional light as the sun.
- **API:** `render.settings rtShadows {enabled, maxLights}` is `rtDirect {enabled, candidates 1-32, spatial 0-8}`.

### T4: tests, docs
- `engine_tests` (`f4_*`): RT direct light shadows every light (castShadows false too) against the loop; a capture
  after a reset exact run to run (realtime and reference); CSM only as the fallback; twelve lights (past the old 8
  channels) within 1.8 levels of reference at 32 frames; realtime frames 1 and 16 within 2 levels of a 256-frame
  reference; settings defaults and switches.
- `light_cluster_gpu_test` runs the loop with RT direct light off; `light_emitter_gpu_test` holds the deferred frame
  past an emitter to the same light (mean within 1%, blocks within 5%), as it did the path tracer.
- AGENTS.md ("RT direct light"), ARCHITECTURE.md (the passes), the CLI and serve guides.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **1/M darkened the frame more every frame.** Spatial reuse counted neighbours whose surface could never have drawn the
  point (the far side of a sphere light, a shadow): Cornell's walls 0.86-0.93 of reference at 30 frames, the box's
  penumbra 0.889 at 16. Z with a visibility ray a neighbour: 0.98-1.0.
- **A shadow 3x too bright.** Inside a shadow, RIS against an unshadowed target sometimes keeps a dim light's point
  carrying the occluded light's weight; spatial output fed back into temporal reuse moved it frame to frame into a
  streak. The boiling filter (20x the tile's mean) and dropping an occluded final point from the kept reservoir fixed it.
- **Mirror reflections smeared:** the a-trous filter spread specular at any roughness.
- **The first implementation cost 3.1 ms of Cornell's frame at 720p** (the full OpenPBR BSDF as the target, the
  G-buffer decoded per neighbour, a 5x5 filter reading position and normal per tap). The cheap target, the compact
  surface, the guide image, a 3x3 filter over three steps, 4 candidates and 2 neighbours: 1.57 ms.

## 4. Spec corrections

- **castShadows is the CSM fallback's only.** RT direct light shadows every light, as the path tracer always has.
- **The sun is a delta** in the deferred frame too (RT shadows gave it an angular radius); its shadows are hard.
- **Shading is at full resolution.** MegaLights resamples and traces at half resolution; this step does not (section 6).

## 6. Known gaps

- **Four gated frame budgets are missed** (cornell_720p, helmet_game_1080p, play_hud_1080p, play_vfx_1080p, above).
  For Frank: rule the new costs into the budgets, or have the next step resample and trace at half resolution as
  MegaLights does (expected about a quarter of `di.sample` and `di.shade`), with the budgets ruled after it.
- **A temporal drift:** realtime is about 1 level (0-255) farther from a converged reference at frame 16 than at frame 1;
  the filters' bias (the boiling filter, the firefly clamp) is 2-4% darker than reference on Cornell.
- **Small mirror highlights** are fainter in realtime than in reference.
- **Rays start 5 cm off the surface**, as RT GI's: within 5 cm of a contact the light can leak.
- **No RT direct light without ray queries** (ruling F-R6: the fallback is the loop and CSM).
