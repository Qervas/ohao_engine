## 1. Summary

- **Alpha modes.** glTF's `alphaMode`, `alphaCutoff` and base colour alpha reach the material table
  (row 3: mode, cutoff, opacity, base alpha); primitives get `MaterialOverride.alphaMode` and
  `opacity`. `MASK` is cut in the G-buffer, RT shadows, RT GI and the path tracer; `BLEND` leaves the
  G-buffer, Hi-Z and CSM, casts no RT shadow and bounces no GI, and is drawn by the forward pass (the
  path tracer blends it by stochastic opacity).
- **The forward pass.** `vfx.expand` evaluates every effect particle in closed form at
  `(step − spawn − 1 + alpha)·dt` and lights a lit one at its centre (clustered lights, one sun ray,
  RT GI or the environment or the constant ambient). `vfx.sort` orders each alpha batch back to front
  by integer keys. `transparent` (after SSR, before bloom) draws blended meshes and effects back to
  front with their own blend states, soft against the G-buffer's depth, and writes a coverage mask TAA
  lowers its history weight under.
- **Effects in every host.** The `vfx_draw` sync system hands the RenderScene the live records:
  windows, captures, `ohao run` and headless sessions draw the same effects. `render/particles`,
  `shaders/particles`, `runtime/combat_fx` and `setParticlesDrawn` are deleted; `--fx`/`--no-fx` are
  removed.

## 2. What was built

- **Materials:** `render::AlphaMode`, `MaterialDesc::{alphaMode, alphaCutoff, opacity, baseAlpha}`,
  `kMaterialRows` 4 (`layout.json`); the loader reads `AI_MATKEY_GLTF_ALPHAMODE`/`ALPHACUTOFF` and the
  base colour's alpha; `MaterialOverride.alphaMode` (`MaterialAlpha`) and `opacity`.
- **G-buffer:** `kDrawMask`/`kDrawTransparent` draw flags; `cull/instances.slang` keeps both out of the
  G-buffer lists and `kDrawTransparent` out of the cascades; `core/gbuffer_mask.frag` (the shared body
  `includes/scene/gbuffer_body.glsl` with a discard) draws the MASK draws after the late lists.
- **RT:** `FORCE_NO_OPAQUE` TLAS instances for alpha materials, `kTlasMaskTransparent` (8) for all-BLEND
  ones; `rt/alpha_test.slang` tests candidates in `shadow_trace`/`gi_trace` (`rtc::AlphaTables`, set 1;
  bindless textures, set 2); `pt_anyhit` does MASK and stochastic BLEND; the raygens force opacity
  while no instance has an alpha material.
- **`render/vfx/vfx_renderer.*`:** the emitter table, batches, host object sort, particle and order
  slots (the set's cap, 16,384), the R8 mask, the `transparent` render pass and pipelines (three
  particle blend modes, blended meshes one- and double-sided through `core/gbuffer.vert`).
- **Shaders:** `includes/lighting/clustered_lights.slang` (the forward copy of the deferred light loop
  and BRDF), `vfx/expand.slang`, `vfx/sort.slang`, `vfx/particle.slang`, `vfx/transparent_mesh.slang`;
  `postprocess/taa_resolve.frag` reads the mask.
- **`ecs/vfx_draw.*`:** `installVfxDraw` (LiveHost, the CLI's render host), `vfxFrameOf`;
  `VfxSystem::setVersion()`/`stepDt()`.
- **Passes:** `vfx.expand`, `vfx.sort`, `transparent` registered; `particles` removed.
  `independentBlend` enabled on the device.
- **Content:** `assets/vfx/alpha_card.gltf` (`tools/vfx/make_alpha_card.py`, byte for byte: a MASK leaf
  card), `assets/vfx/transparency.vfx.json`, `assets/scenes/transparency.scene.json`. The toon enemy's
  rifle materials lost a BLEND mode at alpha 1 (`tools/anim/make_enemy.py` `solid()`; the rifle drew as

- **Tests:** `vfx_gpu_test` (new, GPU); `deferred_determinism_gpu_test` gains `transparency_post`,

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- Slang's unsized resource array (`Sampler2D t[]`) carries an `ArrayStride` spirv-val rejects
  (VUID-StandaloneSpirv-None-10684, 4 messages per renderer in engine_tests): declared at the bindless
  table's size, 4096.
- A two-entry Slang file compiles to `<name>.<entry>.spv`: the particle pipeline loaded a file that did
  not exist.
- `VfxExpandPush` was 192 bytes, past the guaranteed 128: the view matrix became its third row and the
  environment's intensity shares `camera.w`.
- A particle off screen read the froxel of its clamped pixel, which need not hold its lights: it sums
  every light now.
- The rifle's materials were BLEND at alpha 1 in the shipped glb: the play scene's goldens moved until
  the tool and the asset were fixed.
- The parity case first compared a blended closed box: its far faces draw over its near face (no depth
  write between blended faces); a pane is a quad.
