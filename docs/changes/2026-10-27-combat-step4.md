# Combat, step 4: presentation

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-27-combat-design.md` ("Presentation (step 4)", "As built (step 4)",
"Rulings for Frank" 29–36)
**Status:** done 2026-10-27 (plan `docs/plans/2026-10-27-combat.md`, Task 4)

## 1. Summary

A fight now looks and sounds like one. Each round lights the gun's muzzle for 3 steps; every hit
leaves a dark mark on the wall, crate or enemy it struck; the player hears their shots, the
enemies' shots, impacts by surface, being hurt, deaths and reloads; and a HUD shows health,
ammunition, a crosshair opened by bloom, a hit marker, a red vignette when hurt, "DEAD" with the
restart countdown and "CLEARED". The click that takes the mouse back in `ohao_game` no longer
fires a round. Captures draw the HUD only when asked (`hud: true`, `ohao run --hud`), so every
existing golden and capture is unchanged by the HUD.

## 2. What was built

- **Muzzle flash:** `Muzzle {intensity 6, time 0.05}` (saved) marks a `Light` below a weapon;
  `WeaponState.flash` (saved) counts its time down; the combat step's `updateFlash` patches the
  light on for `time` (3 steps at 60 Hz) and back to 0. `render::lightEmitter` gives no emitter
  for a light at intensity ≤ 0. The play scene's player and both enemies carry a gun box and a
  muzzle light.
- **Impact marks** (`ohao/ecs/impact_marks.{hpp,cpp}`): 64 runtime 8 cm quads from `hit` events,
  2 mm off the surface, in the hit entity's space (on a character, the `BoxPrim` child the round
  crossed; none on the player), oldest reused, cleared by a restart; the `impact_marks` sync
  system draws them as RenderScene instances (`installImpactMarks`, in `LiveHost` and the CLI's
  render host). Never in the registry.
- **HUD:** `render::HudList` (`ohao/render/hud/hud_list.{hpp,cpp}`: rectangles, 5×7 glyphs,
  vignette; 48-byte items, 2,048 at most), `HudPass` (`hud_pass.{hpp,cpp}`,
  `shaders/hud/hud.slang`), registered as `hud` after `gizmo`, run only for a non-empty list;
  `ecs::CombatHud` (`ohao/ecs/combat_hud.{hpp,cpp}`) lays it out from combat state and the log;
  `VulkanRenderer::setHud`, `DeferredRenderer::setHud`.
- **Where:** `LiveHost` sets the HUD every frame; `render.capture {hud}` /
  `RenderRequest.hud` / `ohao run --render ... --hud` for captures (deferred frames only).
- **Sounds:** `runtime::soundsFor` and `CombatSounds` (`ohao/runtime/combat_sounds.{hpp,cpp}`),
  played by `LiveHost` beside the footsteps under `LiveConfig::footsteps`; 10 WAVs in
  `assets/audio/weapons/` generated by `tools/audio/make_weapons.py` (ours, MIT, fixed seeds;
  README beside them).
- **The capturing click:** `runtime::ClickToCapture` (`ohao/runtime/click_capture.{hpp,cpp}`)
  swallows a mouse press on a free cursor and its release before the input map;
  `LiveHost::setClickToCapture`/`takeCaptureClick`, `Window::cursorCaptured`; `ohao_game` uses it
  in play mode; the play scene's `capture_mouse` binding is gone.
- **Bench:** `render_bench` scene `play_hud_1080p` and the `hud` budget (0.05 ms, gated from 15).
- **Goldens:** `cli_blockout_play_hud` (new); `cli_blockout_play_deferred` re-baselined.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **`engine_tests` crashed creating the HUD pipeline.** The shader stages named entry point
  `main`, but the Slang build keeps entry point names (`-fvk-use-entrypoint-name`): `vs`/`fs`.
- **A headless host would have missed the fight before its first capture.** Its renderer, and so
  its marks and HUD, start at the first capture; `ImpactMarks` and `CombatHud` read the combat log
  from its oldest kept event on their first observe instead of from "now".
- Tests of mine: the enemy mark test expected the mark on the enemy's +Z face (it faces the
  player along −Z); the restart-clears-marks test pressed twice inside the 10 Hz cooldown and
  fired once.
- **The gun was too big and central** in the first look shots; shrunk to 3.2 × 4.4 × 24 cm and
  moved right and down (`(0.22, −0.21, −0.26)` under the head).

## 4. Spec corrections

- The muzzle is a `Muzzle` component on the light, not `Weapon.muzzle`/`flashIntensity`/
  `flashTime` (ruling 29).
- Impact marks land on enemies too, following them; never on the player (ruling 30).
- A light at intensity 0 has no emitter mesh (ruling 32); the HUD is on deferred frames only
  (ruling 33); the capturing click is the host's, `capture_mouse` is gone (ruling 34).

## 6. Known gaps

- The left wall's end shows a column of bright speckles in the look shots (`step4_fight_01`,
  `_02`) when seen from close by. It is not this step's: the same view captured with the three
  muzzle lights removed is byte for byte the same, and the speckles are there without bloom
  (`build/sp7/debug_speckle.py`). It looks like RT GI/specular fireflies on that face; not
  investigated here.
- The muzzle light lights the nearest wall brightly for its 3 steps (intensity 6 at under 1 m);
  a tuning question, not a defect.
- Marks on enemies are dark on dark red and hard to see at range.
- Path-traced frames have no HUD.
- Combat's agent methods (`combat.info`, `combat.events`, `combat.damage`) are step 5.
