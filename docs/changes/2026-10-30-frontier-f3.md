# Frontier F3: emission is light

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md` (section 3, step F3; ruling F-R7).
**Plan:** `docs/plans/2026-10-30-frontier-f3.md`, T1–T3.
**Status:** built.

## 1. Summary

**Every emissive triangle is a light of the path tracer.** A GPU pass builds, every frame, a table of the triangles
of every emissive material with each one's power; next-event estimation draws among them by power (RTXDI's
hierarchical draw over a power image), weighed against BSDF hits by the power heuristic, so a small bright emitter
(the helmet's visor and panels, a lamp's shade) is found by a shadow ray instead of waiting for a bounce to hit it.

**The emissive proxy is gone.** The deferred frame added a made-up point light per model with an emissive texture,
on top of RT GI's bounce, which already brings back what a hit emits: the model's light counted twice. Its
component, its function and their paths through instantiate, spawn and the extract are deleted.

## 2. What was built

### T1: the table, the sampling, the weights
- **`render/rt/emissive_lights.*` (`EmissiveLights`).** The CPU tracks the instances with an emissive material
  (`MaterialTable::emissive`: emission colour and luminance above 0; visible, not a light's emitter mesh or a view
  model) where alpha instances are tracked (`RenderScene::stageTlasInstance`), and hands the path tracer their
  ranges (`emissiveInstances()`: slot, triangle count; `emissiveVersion()`).
- **`emissive.lights`** (before `pt.cache.update`): `rt/emissive_collect.slang`, one thread a triangle, writes its
  world corners, uvs and material (binding 42, four `uint4` rows: a material index as a float is a denormal, which
  float paths flush) and its power into level 0 of an R32F image; `rt/emissive_reduce.slang` sums the levels
  above 2x2 down to the total (binding 43). Binding 44 holds the counts and the instances' ranges, binding 45
  each instance slot's first light (closest hit).
- **Power** is `pi x luminance(emission_color x emission_luminance x the emissive texture's mean) x area`. The
  texture's mean is one sample at the triangle's uv centroid at the mip whose texels match its uv area (RTXDI's
  estimate); the collect pass reads the path tracer's bindless table.
- **Selection by power.** A light sample draws the analytic lights (uniformly among them) with probability `q`,
  their power over all the power (`RenderScene::analyticLightPower`: a point `4 pi I`, a spot its cone's share,
  an area light `pi L A`, a sun its irradiance over the scene's cross-section), else a triangle by descending the
  mips, then a point uniformly on it, its emission with its texture there, two-sided as the closest hit's.
- **The weight.** A BSDF ray that hits an emissive triangle (`kHitEmissive`, light
  `emissiveFirst[InstanceID()] + PrimitiveIndex()`) is weighed against the light sampling's density there by the
  power heuristic, in the trace and in the radiance cache's update.

### T2: the proxy retired
- **Deleted:** `EmissiveProxyLight` (component and registration), `render::emissiveProxy`, the `EmissiveProxy`
  struct, `SpawnedModel.emissive`, their instantiate, spawn and extract paths.
- `LightKind::EmissiveProxy` is now `LightKind::Flash`: what remains of it is the effects' flashes (raster only,
  no mesh, no shadow), which `vfx_draw` writes.
- `ecs_tests`' proxy cases became `emissive_texture_adds_no_raster_light`.

### T3: close
- `check_analytic` gained `emissive_quad`; every scene there now says `Environment {sky: false}` (the physical
  sky, F2, would light them otherwise).
- AGENTS.md ("Emission is light"; the ECS writes gotcha; the radiance cache's floor), ARCHITECTURE.md (the pass
  count, 39; the path-traced frame's stages), the plan, the spec's F3 row, ROADMAP.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **The material table was bound to the hit stages only** (binding 10). The raygen's light sampling read emission
  from it: every emissive light sampled black until it was bound to RGEN too.
- **A triangle in the shading point's own plane faced it.** Light samples were drawn from the shading point but
  traced from the offset shadow-ray origin; a wall's own triangles, at cosine 0 from the point, were taken as
  facing it. Samples are now drawn from the shadow ray's origin.
- **The whole helmet drew samples.** Power without the emissive texture counted every triangle of the helmet (its
  material's emission is white; the texture is black over most of it): the helmet took samples from the room's
  point lights and the walls got noisier. The texture's mean fixes most of it (section 5).
- **`check_material`'s repeat ran in two processes.** Its realtime closed box was the same bits only because every
  path there contributed `Le x 0.5^k`, independent of where it hit. With light sampling a contribution depends on
  positions, the driver's per-process BVH can move a shared-edge hit by an ulp, and one flipped rounding of the
  radiance cache's integer sums spreads over the frame (3 of 30 processes, 0.25% mean). The check now captures
  twice in one process (`ohao run --script`): 15 of 15 the same bits. Offline, the same floor moved 1 run in 30 by
  at most 1.4e-5.

## 4. Spec corrections

- **Selection is by power, not half and half.** The plan's `q = 1/2` was noisier on the helmet than `q` by power.
- **The deferred frame does not select emissive triangles itself.** Its emissive light reaches surfaces through RT
  GI's bounce (the hit's emission), as the path tracer's does past the first vertex; the spec's F3 row said both
  renderers select them. Direct emissive light in deferred comes with F4's ReSTIR DI.

## 5. Results

- **The referee.** `check_analytic` 16/16: `emissive_quad` (a 1 x 1 m emissive rectangle 1 m above the floor,
  radiance 20, the floor probed 0.6 m from its edge, against the rectangle's form factor) path traced x1.014,
  deferred at reference quality x1.006. `check_material` 21/21: the closed box with every wall a light 2.0000 /
  1.7500 / 1.5000 (all bounces, 1, 0; exact to 4 places), realtime 1.9720 (x0.986, the cache's bias), deferred
  1.5029. `check_validation` 19/19 clean.
- **Noise** (the helmet room, the `damaged_helmet` golden's 16 samples against 1,024, both box-filtered to
  640x360, RMSE in 8-bit codes):

  | | whole frame | brightest 3% |
  |---|---|---|
  | before F3 (BSDF hits only) | 18.38 | 1.80 |
  | power, texture left out | 20.83 | 1.33 |
  | power with the texture's mean (built) | 19.75 | 0.88 |

  The emitters' error halves; the whole frame is 7% worse, because selection by power ignores distance and the
  panels still draw some samples the walls' point lights would use (F4).
- **Cost** (`render_bench`, 1080p, medians): `emissive.lights` 0.007 ms on the helmet room, 0.00003 ms on the play
  scene (no emissive material); `pt.trace` 15.76 ms on the helmet room and 14.90 ms on the play scene (F1: 16.3 and
  15.7, this machine's spread). No budget proposed: the pass is 0.04% of the frame.
- **Gate:** `tools/check.py` 56 tests, 55 passed (the repeat in `check_material`, section 3), then 21/21 after
  the fix. Suites touched: `engine_tests` 87, `ecs_tests` 36, `api_tests` 70, `scene_tests` 84 (the catalogue
  golden without the proxy's component). CLI and serve expectations: the schema hash only.
- **Goldens:** 26 of 32 unchanged. `damaged_helmet` and `cli_damaged_helmet` (path traced: the sample sequence;
  max_abs 19 on 3.1%, scattered, no structure) and the four helmet deferred scenes (the proxy's light gone from the
  helmet's rims and glints: max_abs 30 on 0.7%, all on the helmet) were looked at and re-captured.

## 6. Known gaps

- **Selection ignores distance and orientation** (power only): F4's ReSTIR DI chooses by what a light gives the
  point.
- **Realtime GI's denoiser loses 15% on small bright emitters** (`emissive_quad` deferred at realtime quality
  x0.851); F5.
- **Without ray queries emission lights nothing in deferred** (ruling F-R6: the fallback is kept correct, not
  extended).
- **The texture's mean is one sample**: a triangle much larger than its texture's detail is estimated from one mip
  texel; an estimate only moves noise, never the mean (a triangle estimated 0 is never drawn and its hits count in
  full).
