## 1. Summary

- **`audio.info {since, max}`** reads the voice schedule in any host: the round's snapshot and its
  blend, the listener's zone, every bus (level, cap, voices held), voice and ray counts, and the
  commands after a cursor. Headless it answers `device: "none"`; a window with a sound device adds
  its sample rate, late commands, the mix's cost and each bus's driven level and sounding voices.
- **`audio.mix {bus, db, mute, solo, snapshot, clear}`** sets listening overrides the session
  keeps and the window's player applies in the device only: never the world, the schedule, a
  snapshot or `world.hash`.
- **`render.stats.audio {mixMs, voices, late}`** in a window with sound; `ohao serve --window
  --sound` opens a device.
- **Budgets:** `serve_bench audio_info_bytes` (2 KB). `tools/list` stays under its 24 KB cap after
  trimming method descriptions (OPEN 16 not needed).
- **Docs:** AGENTS.md, ARCHITECTURE.md (the player's overrides, a recipe "add a sound"), the CLI and
  serve guides (methods, a "Listen" section with a checked example), the monograph (`audio/system`
  gains the reverb and the mix meter; a new `audio/schedule` unit).

## 2. What was built

- `ohao/api/audio.cpp`: `audio.info` and `audio.mix`; params `AudioInfoParams` (`since` ≥ 0, `max`
  0–64, default 16) and `AudioMixParams` (`db` −96..12); dispatch and docs in `session.cpp`.
- `api::Host::audioDevice()` and `Host::setAudioMix()`; `RenderStats::Audio`; `LiveHost` fills
  them from its `AudioPlayer` and `AudioSystem`.
- `runtime::AudioPlayer`: `MixOverrides` (per bus db, mute, solo; a forced snapshot), applied in
  the device and again on a bank load; `deviceBuses()` (each bus's driven level, mute, solo,
  sounding voices).
- `AudioSystem::mixMs()`: the engine is allocated beside a ring of 512 period timings; the device
  callback (ours, around `ma_engine_read_pcm_frames`) times each period, scaled to 10 ms of audio;
  `render()` without a device pushes into the same ring.
- `AudioSchedule::busDuckDb()` and `snapshotBlendSteps()` for `audio.info`.
- `tools/ohao`: `serve --window --sound`.
- Tests: `api_tests` `t29_audio_info`, `t29_audio_mix`; `tests/serve/sessions/audio.jsonl` and its
  expectation; `check_live` test 29 (a realtime window with `--sound`: `render.stats.audio`,
  `audio.info`'s device half, a mute; a window without sound: none); `e2e_mcp`'s audio leg (the
  newest commands mid-fight, a mute, a solo, `clear`, `world_hash` unchanged); `serve_bench`
  `audio_info_bytes`.
- Docs and monograph as in section 1; `docs/agents/examples/audio.jsonl`.
