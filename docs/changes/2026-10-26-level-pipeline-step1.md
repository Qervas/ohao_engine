# Level pipeline, step 1: assets and staging

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-26-level-pipeline-design.md` ("Assets and staging", "Extras:
the hint schema", "Build order" step 1, test 1); plan
`docs/plans/2026-10-26-level-pipeline.md`, Task 1.

**Status:** built and tested.

## 1. Summary

**What step 1 delivers:**
- A checked-in Blender blockout level, generated by a script so it is ours (MIT, as the
  repo):
  - floor, walls, a doorway, a step, a platform, a ramp, a pillar;
  - two dynamic crates, three spawn points and a camera;
  - every level hint written as a node `extras` custom property.
- It loads and renders through today's `ModelAsset` path, with an exact golden.
- A check holds its hints to the spec's schema and its bytes to its Blender rebuild.
- Frank's Somnabulate Flat42 props are staged as GLB into a git-ignored folder:
  - materials rebuilt from their textures;
  - normal maps flipped to OpenGL's convention;
  - Unreal's `UCX_` hulls and `SOCKET_` empties kept as hints.
- The props render textured in the blockout.

**What step 1 does not deliver:** no engine code changed. The level loader, collision and
the agent surface are steps 2–5.

## 2. What was built

**The blockout:**
- `tools/level/blockout_room.py` (Blender 5.2.2, headless) writes
  `assets/levels/blockout_room.glb`: 16,804 bytes, 17 nodes, 13 meshes, 4 materials.
- Hints, as node `extras`:
  - `col: box` on the floor, walls, step, platform and pillar, `col: mesh` on the ramp;
  - `body: dynamic` with `mass` on the two crates;
  - `spawn: player` and 2 × `spawn: enemy` on empties.
- 16 hinted nodes carry 13–42 bytes of `extras` each, 279 bytes in all.
- Two Blender runs write identical bytes.

**Repository files:**
- `assets/levels/README.md` (provenance, license, rebuild); `.gitattributes`:
  `assets/levels/** -text`.
- `assets/scenes/blockout_room.scene.json`, canonical per `ohao fmt`: a camera inside the
  room, the outdoor HDRI, a sun, a point lamp, and the level as one `ModelAsset` entity.
- `tests/level/extras_schema.json`: the hint keys as JSON Schema (`col`, `body`, `mass`,
  `fric`, `rest`, `vis`, `spawn`, `socket`).

**`tests/level/check_level_assets.py`** (CTest `check_level_assets`, label `quick`), four
tests:
- every checked-in level's `extras` validate;
- the blockout's node names, translations and hints are the contract later steps assert
  against;
- a Blender rebuild is byte-identical (`skip` without Blender 5.2.2);
- the scene references the level.

**`tests/golden/manifest.json`:** `cli_blockout_room_deferred` (30 frames, TAA, SSAO, bloom,
exposure 1), exact.

**`tools/level/stage_flat42.py`** (Blender) converts `SM_<Prop>.fbx` and
`T_<Prop>_BC/N/ORM.png` from Somnabulate's Flat42 folder (read only) to
`assets/local/flat42/<Prop>.glb`:
- base colour, ORM as glTF's metallic-roughness and occlusion, and the normal map with green
  flipped (Unreal's are DirectX);
- `UCX_` hulls with `{"col":"convex","vis":0}`, the visual mesh `{"col":"none"}`, `SOCKET_`
  empties as `{"socket":…}`;
- `--visual-only` writes `<Prop>_visual.glb` without hulls or sockets, which is what today's
  merged path can draw.

**Staging:**
- `assets/local/` is git-ignored except its README, which states the staging rule.
- Staged: PinnChair (1,224 KB), KitchenTable (4,470 KB), BookStack (972 KB) and Kettle
  (649 KB), plus their visual-only variants.
- Four local scenes furnish the blockout with them (wide and close, full and visual-only).

**AGENTS.md:** the new suite (CPU list and table), the golden count (25), and a gotcha on
levels and staged assets.

**Spec and plan** (committed before the step): `fefda8c`, with the roadmap row linking both.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

**Found, not fixed** (step 2's, recorded in the spec's "Why" and in plan Task 2 step 5):
- `ModelLoader::load` runs `repairMeshNormals` on every model.
- It merges vertices by position and overwrites the file's normals with smoothed ones.
- On the blockout it changed 263 of 306 normals: the boxes render with rounded shading.
- On the Flat42 props it changed 942–1,388 normals each.
- Every golden over a model with authored hard edges depends on it, so fixing it moves
  goldens.

## 4. Spec corrections

- The "Why" section gained the normal-repair finding, and step 2 gained its fix.
- The "Size" line was replaced with the measured numbers above. The first draft said 15
  nodes and 317 bytes, which was not a measurement.

## 6. Known gaps

- **Loading:** levels load only as one merged mesh. No per-node entities, `extras`, lights
  or cameras yet (steps 2–3).
- **Normals:** authored normals are smoothed (above, step 2).
- **Collision** does not exist yet (step 4).
- **Somnabulate assets are not in git.** Committing them waits for Frank's ruling (spec
  Open decision 1), since the repo is MIT and has a GitHub origin. A fresh clone has
  `assets/local/README.md` and the command that stages them.
- **LODs:** Flat42's `_LOD1` files are not staged.
- **Ready GLBs:** the ones in `games/generated/` (2–20 MB with baked 4K textures) are not
  staged.
- **Blockout folders:** `.delilah-preview/d1_blockout` and `bench04/pass11/flat_blockout`
  hold only reference renders, no geometry.
- **The exporter warns** "More than one shader node tex image used for a texture" for the
  ORM image, which feeds both metallic-roughness and occlusion. The exported materials
  still carry all four texture slots (checked per prop).
