## 1. Summary

Effects are now data and simulation:
- **The set:** one `.vfx.json` per world, named by a new saved singleton `VfxSet`: effects (emitters
  of closed-form particles), decals, tracers, which combat events spawn them, and caps. The parser
  reports every error at its JSON pointer. Without a `VfxSet`, the default set holds today's muzzle,
  sparks and dust by surface as data.
- **The step:** a `vfx` simulate system writes spawn records (effect, tracer, decal, light) from the
  step's `shot`, `hit` and `explode`, and from placed `VfxEmitter`s by step count. Records are
  runtime: never saved or hashed, carried by snapshots, the same in every host.
- **The closed form:** a particle's position, size, colour and alpha are a function of (record,
  index, age) in `ohao/vfx/particle_math.hpp`, with counter-based randomness. Step 2's shader repeats
  it.
- **Agents:** `vfx.info {since, max}` reads the set, the counts and the records after a cursor.

`world.hash` after a fight is the same with and without the vfx system. The expectations that moved
are hashes, the method count, and the play scene's snapshot growing by its `VfxSet`.

## 2. What was built

- `ohao/vfx/` (new library `ohao_vfx`, no ECS, no Vulkan):
  - `vfx_set.{hpp,cpp}`: `parseVfxSet` (simdjson), `defaultVfxSet()`, `vfxSetJsonSchema()`
    (`ohao schema vfx-set [-o file]`), `resolveEffect`/`resolveDecal` for `{surface}` patterns with
    a fallback.
  - `particle_math.hpp`: `vfxHash(seed, index, attribute)`, `vfxUnit`, curves, the ballistic closed
    form with drag and gravity (and the k = 0 branch), the cone direction, `evaluate(emitter, seed,
    index, age, pose, frames)`.
- Components: `VfxSet { asset }` (saved singleton) and `VfxEmitter { effect, rate, seed, active }`
  (saved; `rate` in Hz, 0–1000), with docs, units, limits and writers.
- `ohao/ecs/vfx.{hpp,cpp}`: `installVfx(world, assets)` (the CLI context and `LiveHost`, after
  `installAudio`) adds the `vfx` simulate system before `audio_schedule` through the new
  `World::addSimulateBefore`, and chains a `SnapshotHook` in front of the schedule's. Each step it
  loads the named set (`W_VFX_SET` and the default set if it cannot), reads the combat log after the
  step's last `restart`, writes records (muzzle effect at the logical eye + 0.5 m; a tracer by
  `everyPlayer`/`everyEnemy` cadence to the shooter's next hit, else 60 m, none under
  `minDistance`; impact effect and decal by surface; the grenade's boom, smoke and scorch), steps
  placed emitters by step count, expires records past their life and drops the oldest over a cap by
  kind, counted. Seeds: `vfxHash(vfx step, 8 × event index + part, 0xEF7)`.
- `ohao/api/vfx.cpp`: `vfx.info` (the step half; `particles: null` until step 2's frame half).
- `assets/vfx/slice.vfx.json` (first version: today's presets with linear colours; impact dust,
  `grenade_boom` with a 0.12 s light, `smoke_cloud` with a proxy; bullet decals per surface and the
  scorch; the rifle tracer), named by `assets/scenes/blockout_play.scene.json`'s `VfxSet`.
- Log channel `vfx` (`core/log`, and `log.read`'s channel names).
- `physics_bench vfx_step_ms` (the play scene's scripted fight), gated by `vfxStep` 1 at 0.02 ms.
- `tests/vfx/vfx_tests.cpp` (test 31) and `api_tests` `t35_vfx_info_step`.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **A failed set re-read its file every step:** `refreshSet` compared the named path with the loaded
  one, which stays empty after a failure. It now remembers the named path separately.
- **`t31_replay`'s first snapshot held a grenade in flight** (step 130); a grenade in flight is
  combat's runtime state, never snapshotted (audio ruling 23), so the restored fight never exploded.
  The test snapshots after the explosion (step 200).
- **`VfxEmitter.rate` in `1/s`** failed the registry's unit lint; it is `Hz`.
- **`e2e_mcp` counted 38 tools**; it counts 39 with `vfx_info`.

## 4. Spec corrections

All in the spec's "As built (step 1)":
- Seeds from the vfx step and the event's index after the step's last `restart`, not the event's
  `seq` (not part of a snapshot or a restart).
- The muzzle at the logical eye + 0.5 m: the `Muzzle` entity is placed by the pose system, which is
  presentation.
- Decals attach to the hit's target by its pose at the step, without the body-box re-cast (step 3).
- A restart puts back the round start's records (none), so the steps after it write the round's
  records again.
- `vfx.info` holds 16 records in 2 KB (measured 1,593 bytes), not 1 KB.
- `render_host` installs nothing: the CLI context installs the simulation systems.
- The closed form is checked against RK4 at 1 ms, not Euler (Euler's own error under gravity is
  g·T·dt/2 = 9.8 mm over 2 s).
- `t31_window_equals_headless` and an `input.replay` of a fight through `vfx.info` move to step 4's
  GPU harness legs.
