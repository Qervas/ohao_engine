# Frontier F2: the display chain

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md` (section 3, step F2; rulings F-R4 and F-R5).
**Plan:** `docs/plans/2026-10-30-frontier-f2.md`, T1–T5.
**Status:** built.

## 1. Summary

**Every frame becomes pixels through one display transform** (`shaders/display/display.slang`):
- exposure from a physical camera's EV100 (Lagarde and de Rousiers 2014): radiance `L` shows as `L / (1.2 x 2^EV100)`;
- AgX (Sobotka, as fit by Wrensch's minimal AgX) by default, Khronos PBR Neutral selectable;
- the sRGB transfer function.

The deferred post, the path tracer's output, NRD's frame, DLSS's frame and OIDN's CPU frames all use it, at the same
EV100 and curve. Four copies of the ACES fit, three other tonemaps, `pow(1/2.2)` and the path tracer's fixed exposure of
0.5 are gone.

## 2. What was built

### T1: one display transform
- **The module** `shaders/display/display.slang`: `displayExposure(ev100)`, `agx`, `pbrNeutral`, `srgbEncode`,
  `displayPixel(radiance, exposure, curve)`.
- **Its users, all Slang now:**
  - `postprocess/tonemap.slang` (the deferred post's display pass; bloom added before it);
  - `rt/path_tracer.slang` (its output image; the exposure and curve in `pc.tuning.zw`);
  - `rt/nrd_compose.slang` and `rt/dlss_display.slang` (push constants: exposure, curve).
- **One owner.** The post stack holds EV100 and the curve (`setEv100`, `setDisplayCurve`); the renderer hands them to
  the path tracer every frame (`setDisplay`), so a capture in any mode displays alike.
- **OIDN's frames** go through the C++ twin `render/display/display.hpp`. `display_test` runs the module on the CPU
  through its slangc C++ build (`tools/display`, as `tools/sampling` does for Sobol) and holds the twin to it.
- **The API:** `render.settings` and `render.capture` take `ev100` (default 0.5, EV at ISO 100; reflection's new unit
  `EV`) and `tonemap` (`agx` | `neutral`) in place of `exposure`; `ohao run --ev100 EV --tonemap agx|neutral` in place
  of `--exposure`. A capture's EV100 and curve now apply to path-traced captures too (the exposure applied to deferred
  ones only).

**Deleted:**
- `postprocess/tonemapping.frag` (the ACES fit, Reinhard, Uncharted 2, a "filmic" curve, a PBR Neutral without its
  toe, a gamma), `TonemapOperator`, `setGamma` and the unused lightning flash (`setFlashIntensity`, no caller);
- `compute/dlss_tonemap.comp`, `rt/nrd_compose.comp` and `includes/rt/nrd_frontend.glsl` (ported);
- the path tracer's `aces()` and OIDN's CPU ACES.

### T2: auto exposure
- **`postprocess/exposure.slang`** (the `exposure` pass, before `post.tonemap`): `histogram` bins every pixel's
  log2 luminance into 128 bins over [-10, 20], each holding its count and the exact sum of its pixels' log2
  luminance in integers (1/16 EV: atomics in any order give the same bits); `adapt` (one thread) takes the mean
  between the 50th and 95th percentiles (a straddling bin in proportion), meters it to middle grey 0.18
  (`EV100 = mean + log2(1 / (0.18 x 1.2)) - compensation`), eases toward it (3/s brighter, 1/s darker; at once
  after a reset), and clears the bins.
- **`render/deferred/auto_exposure.*`**: the buffers, the per-slot readback of the EV100 and the barriers; the
  display pass reads the EV100 from the GPU (binding 2), never through the host.
- **Settings:** `render.settings autoExposure` and `exposureCompensation` (EV); `render.stats ev100` (the newest
  finished frame's). A window meters (`LiveConfig::autoExposure`) and passes its frame time; a capture turns
  metering off and a window restores it afterwards, so captures stay at their fixed EV100.

### T3: the physical sky
- **`render/sky/atmosphere.*`** (Hillaire, EGSR 2020, on the CPU, Earth's atmosphere in RGB: Rayleigh, Mie,
  ozone, Hillaire's coefficients): a transmittance table (256x64, Bruneton's parametrisation), the multiple
  scattering table (32x32: 64 directions a texel, L2 / (1 - f_ms)), and a ray march from 200 m up with single
  scattering, the multiple scattering term and a Lambertian ground (albedo 0.3). The tables build in about 15 ms.
- **It lights through the HDRI's paths.** `Environment.sky` (default true): a scene without an HDRI renders the sky
  for its first directional light (colour x intensity as the sun's illuminance outside the atmosphere) into a
  512x256 equirectangular map and uploads it as an HDRI (the renderer's `environmentKey()`), so the CDF, the IBL
  prefilter, RT GI's misses and the path tracer's misses use it unchanged. A world without an `Environment` has the
  sky. No sun, or `sky: false`: no environment.
- **An environment that goes away is unloaded** (the renderer kept the last HDRI before), and the deferred frame is
  told so.
- **Deleted:** `SkyPass` and `postprocess/sky.frag` (Preetham with a procedural night: moon, stars), the `sky`
  pass (and its Cornell budget of 0.03 ms: the pass is gone), the night and moon state, `setSkyEnabled`,
  `setSkyTurbidity`, `setSkyIntensity`, `setSkyGroundColor`.

### T4: bloom by energy
- **No threshold, no clamp.** `postprocess/bloom_input.slang` copies the frame into the pyramid's level 0 (finite,
  non-negative); the downsample chain (Karis's average on the first) and the tent upsample chain are unchanged.
- **Normalised and mixed.** The upsample chain adds each level to the one above at half weight, so level 0 holds
  a convolution of weight `1 + 1/2 + ...` (`BloomPass::levelWeightSum`); the display pass shows
  `(1 - 0.04) frame + 0.04 bloom / weight`, so energy moves and none is added.
- **Deleted:** `bloom_threshold.frag`, `kBloomMaxLuminance`, the threshold, soft knee and intensity settings and
  `setBloomThreshold`/`setBloomIntensity` (no caller).

### T5: close
- AGENTS.md (the display transform, auto exposure, the physical sky, bloom), ARCHITECTURE.md, the CLI and serve
  guides, this record, the goldens.
- **Budgets:** none proposed. `exposure` runs in windows only (`render_bench` captures never meter) and the sky is
  CPU work at a sun's change; `render_bench` has no window scene to measure them.

## 3. Bugs found and fixed

- **The two renderers displayed at different exposures:** deferred at the capture's (default 0.6), the path tracer
  and its denoisers at a fixed 0.5. A capture's `exposure` changed deferred frames only.
- **OIDN's frames truncated to 8 bits** where the GPU paths round: `displayByte` rounds as a UNORM store does.
- **T3: the two renderers showed different skies.** Without an HDRI the deferred frame drew a Preetham sky that lit
  nothing, and the path tracer drew black. Both now show, and are lit by, the same sky (or, without a sun, none).

## 4. Spec corrections

- The plan's T2 shape for `render.settings exposure` (an object with a mode) becomes `ev100` plus T2's own members.
- **The constant ambient moves to F5.** It is what lights a frame without RT GI and without an environment, and the
  SSAO tests stand on it; F5 retires SSAO and one-bounce GI with it.
- **Aerial perspective is not built in T3.** The test scenes are under 100 m deep, where it does not show; it comes
  with a scene that needs it.

## 5. Results

- **`display_test`** (5): the twin equals the module to the 8-bit code and within 2e-6 over five decades of radiance,
  both curves and three exposures; EV100 0 is 1/1.2; the sRGB encoding at 0.04, 0.18 and 0.5; PBR Neutral returns a
  base colour less its 0.04 offset; AgX is grey-neutral, monotonic, from black to white.
- **Goldens:** all 32 re-captured and looked at. AgX is flatter than the ACES fit and takes bright lights toward white:
  Cornell's yellow lamp is the largest change (max_abs 188); the rest is the curve, with no structure in the heat maps.
- **CLI and serve expectations:** the schema hash (the new unit) and `render.settings`' `ev100`/`tonemap` only.

- **`exposure_gpu_test`** (6, under the validation layer and its synchronization checks, clean): Cornell meters at
  EV100 -0.419; every light doubled meters +1.0012 EV; +1 EV of compensation meters -0.9999 EV; a capture after
  metering equals the capture before it byte for byte; a capture leaves the renderer unmetered.

- **`sky_test`** (6): the zenith transmittance of a pure Rayleigh atmosphere within 2e-3 of
  `exp(-beta H (1 - e^(-(top - bottom) / H)))`; the ground blocks a sun below the horizon; noon blue above and paler
  at the horizon; sunset red toward the sun and blue at the zenith; linear in the sun; dark with the sun 20 degrees
  down; the map's orientation (row 0 the zenith, the environment's u).
- **Bloom (T4, `engine_tests`):** a frame filled by one radiance is the same with bloom on and off (max difference
  0); a bright emitter on black glares onto the black beside it with bloom on (6 levels 20 px out) and not with it
  off. The 20 goldens with bloom moved, looked at: the thresholded bloom's wide halos and haze are gone.
- **Goldens (T3):** `anim_rig`'s three (its sun now brings the sky: deferred max_abs 78, path traced 125) and
  `area_light`'s two (its Preetham corners now black, 2.7%), looked at; the other 27 unchanged.

## 6. Known gaps

- **The sun's light keeps its authored colour.** The atmosphere does not yet redden a low sun's direct light (its
  transmittance); the sky beside it is red.
- **A moving sun re-renders the sky** with the device idle (about 30 ms): a day cycle stutters.

- **A realtime path-traced window is not metered:** its frame is displayed by the tracer's own passes (the output,
  NRD's and DLSS's), which read a fixed exposure.

- **AgX's base look is flat** next to the ACES fit. A "punchy" look (Wrensch: saturation and power after the curve) is
  not built; it is a ruling for Frank if the base look is not wanted.
- **The referee's PNGs** (`check_parity`'s error images) are displayed with AgX; their numbers are linear and did not
  move.
