# Frontier renderer F0: dead code out, the scene's sun in

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-30-frontier-renderer-design.md` (section 3, step F0; rulings F-R1–F-R8 open).
**Status:** built. Gate 54 of 54 (quick, gpu, docs); goldens 32 of 32, two moved (looked at).

## 1. Summary

The first step of retiring the renderer's legacy, and the only one that needs no ruling: code that nothing builds,
includes or calls is deleted. One live bug the inventory found is fixed: the deferred frame's CSM fallback and its
procedural sky took their sun from a straight-down default that nothing outside the deferred renderer ever changed.

## 2. What was deleted

| What | Why it was dead |
|---|---|
| `shaders/includes/common/` (`color`, `common`, `constants`, `encoding`, `math`, `reconstruction`), 573 lines | no includer; their headers named passes that no longer exist |
| `shaders/includes/lighting/light_attenuation.glsl`, 149 lines | no includer; the falloff lives in `clustered_lights.slang` |
| `shaders/rt/includes/pbr_unpack.glsl` | a byte-identical copy of `shaders/includes/pbr_unpack.glsl` with no includer |
| `cmake/GenerateShaderConstants.cmake` | called by nothing; read and wrote files that no longer exist |
| the `includes/water/*.glsl` glob line | the directory is gone |
| `ohao/gpu/vulkan/material.*`, `material_instance.*`, 1,035 lines | no engine user; the model loader included `material.hpp` without using it, and one engine test checked a flag enum |
| `ohao/render/async/async_compute_queue.*`, 547 lines | no caller in the engine |
| `tests/renderer/renderer_pipeline_tests.cpp` | in no CMakeLists, never built |
| `tests/python/renderer_bindings.cpp`, `test_renderer.py` | off by default, in no gate, and reported fixed capabilities; the physics bindings stay (no longer linking the renderer) |
| `DeferredRenderer::setDirectionalLight` | no caller |

The monograph lost two units that described only deleted code (`graph/async-compute`, `shaders/includes-common`), along
with their figure and the unreferenced `path-tracer-raygen-dual-path.svg`. Twelve other units were rewritten to the
tree as it is.

## 3. Bug found and fixed

**The sun of the CSM fallback and the procedural sky was straight down.** `DeferredRenderer::m_lightDirection` starts
at (0, −1, 0), and nothing outside the renderer called the setters that change it.

- The CSM fallback (RT shadows off, or no `VK_KHR_ray_query`) projected its shadows from above, whatever the scene's sun
  did.
- The Preetham sky drew its sun overhead.

`setLights`, called each frame with the raster lights, now takes the first directional light and sends it through
`setSunDirection`. Test: `engine_tests` "the scene's directional light is the CSM fallback's and the sky's sun" (a
tilted sun; before the fix the renderer reported the default).

## 4. Results

- Gate: `tools/check.py` passed 54 of 54. `engine_tests` 87/87.
- Goldens: 32 of 32 after two updates. `cli_anim_rig_deferred` and `cli_anim_rig_reference` (max_abs 34 on 46%, rmse
  10.6) moved: their sky is deeper blue away from the scene's tilted sun. Their floor and rig are unchanged except where
  SSR, TAA and bloom read the sky. Both were looked at before the update.
- Correction: `docs/changes/2026-10-29-openpbr-step1.md` said the old material classes stayed because the model loader
  used them. It did not; that line now says so.

## 5. Known gaps

The inventory behind the spec (section 1 there) lists the legacy that needs rulings: the second path tracer, the
non-physical fills, the display chain, emissive proxies, the light loop, one-bounce GI and the GLSL stages. Steps F1–F6
retire them.

Monograph figures still to redraw: `deferred-gbuffer-channels.svg`, `pt_bindings.svg`, `materials-lights-packing.svg`.
