# The player's arms and rifle: how `fps_arms.glb` is made

There is no upstream: no CC0 first-person arms rig was staged for sub-project 10, so the file is
the plan's fallback, generated by `tools/anim/make_fps_arms.py` (ours, MIT) in Blender 5.2.2 LTS
(d13f752e3b9c) in background mode, from an empty factory scene. The rifle is the script's own
blockout (receiver, handguard, barrel, stock, grip, sight, magazine as boxes and tubes), not the
Quaternius AK the enemies carry: that mesh is skinned to the enemy's `Index1.R` frame and has
no separate magazine to pull.

Rebuild (two runs write the same bytes; `tests/level/check_level_assets.py`,
`a3_fps_arms_rebuild_byte_identical`, rebuilds it and compares, skipping without Blender 5.2.2):

```
C:/Users/djmax/scoop/shims/blender.exe -b --factory-startup --python tools/anim/make_fps_arms.py -- assets/characters/fps_arms/fps_arms.glb
```

- **Space:** authored in the view's space with the root at the eye (Blender +Y forward, +Z up;
  the glTF export turns it into the engine's -Z forward, +Y up). The play scene puts it under the
  view model's root, so the remap places it.
- **Mesh** `Arms`, 4 materials (Sleeve, Glove, Gunmetal, Polymer; flat colour, no textures), on
  the skin `ArmsRig`: joints `root`, `gun` (the rifle and the right arm, rigid), `mag` (the
  magazine), `upper_L` (the left upper arm, pivot at the shoulder), `fore_L` (the left forearm
  and hand, pivot at the elbow). Every vertex is weighted wholly to one joint.
- **Clips** (60 fps, linear keys):

| Clip | Length | What |
|---|---|---|
| `rest` | 1 s | the bind pose, held (the additive layer's zero) |
| `idle` | 2 s | a slow breath: 4 mm down, 0.6° pitch |
| `fire` | 0.15 s | the rifle punched 35 mm back, 4 mm and 3° up, and back by 0.15 s |
| `reload` | 2 s | the rifle rolls 25° and dips; the left hand pulls the magazine (out from 0.6 s), drops out of view, brings a new one (home at 1.5 s), and returns |
| `ads_in` | 0.2 s | from the hip to the sights |
| `ads_hold` | 1 s | the sights at the eye, held |
| `sprint` | 0.6 s | the rifle turned down and across, a small bounce |

The reload's events (`mag_out` 0.6 s, `mag_in` 1.5 s) are in `assets/anim/arms.animgraph.json`,
which plays the clip at the weapon's `reloadTime` so `mag_in` lands on the weapon's `reloaded`.
